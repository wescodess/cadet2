# cadet2

cadet2 connects licensed immigration practices and their clients through intake, professional review, and signed plans. Clients also have free non-advice utilities.

## Language

**Practice**:
The immigration consulting or law firm that pays for cadet2 and handles its clients' matters.
_Avoid_: Tenant, agency, paying client

**Representative**:
A licensed RCIC or lawyer who advises a Client and is the only role permitted to sign a plan. The UI says "representative", never "agent".
_Avoid_: Agent, advisor role, consultant role

**Staff**:
A Practice member who prepares work for a Representative and cannot sign a plan.
_Avoid_: Agent, signer, representative

**Practice administrator**:
A Practice member who manages its membership and invitations. This responsibility alone gives neither access to Case contents nor authority to sign.
_Avoid_: Practice owner role, tenant admin, signer

**cadet2 admin**:
A person appointed to administer cadet2 and check Representatives' licences. This role does not make them a Representative or grant general access to Clients' matters.
_Avoid_: Practice administrator, superuser, representative

**Client**:
The applicant who owns their account and profile, whether using cadet2 alone or working with a Practice.
_Avoid_: Customer, lead

**Tracked date**:
A date a Client records for reference or reminders, with its source stated. It is not a determination of immigration status, legal rights or a required action.
_Avoid_: Calculated legal deadline, status guarantee

**Document checklist**:
A sourced list a Client uses to organize documents, independently or for a Case. Completing it does not establish that the evidence is sufficient or an application is ready.
_Avoid_: Eligibility checklist, application approval, submission-ready score

**Vault document**:
A Client-owned file kept privately and shared into a Case only by the Client's explicit selection. It is separate from the Practice-retained record.
_Avoid_: Practice-owned attachment, permanent Case archive

**Case**:
One Client's immigration matter at one Practice.
_Avoid_: Account, profile, household

**Case assignment**:
A Practice member's explicit responsibility for specified work on one Case. It grants no authority on another Case and does not replace the Client's Consent.
_Avoid_: Client ownership, blanket caseload access

**Draft assessment**:
System output prepared for a Practice's review and editing, never shown to the Client unsigned.
_Avoid_: Client recommendation, approved plan, Signed plan

**Signed plan**:
The personalized output a Client receives after a Representative reviews and signs it under their licence.
_Avoid_: Draft assessment, automated advice, Verified Plan

**Plan signature**:
A Representative's recorded adoption of one exact plan revision and its stated basis for one Case. It identifies the signer and preserves what they signed even when their later licence status changes.
_Avoid_: Admin approval, Client consent, eligibility verification

**Superseding plan**:
A newly signed revision that explicitly replaces an earlier Signed plan for the same Case. The earlier plan remains part of the historical record.
_Avoid_: Overwritten plan, refreshed signature, silent correction

**Consent**:
A Client's permission to share information for a particular Case, revocable by the Client. Revocation does not erase the Practice's retained Signed plans and audit record.
_Avoid_: Blanket permission, permanent access

**Practice-retained record**:
The fixed Signed plan and limited evidence of its preparation, signature, disclosure and subsequent custody that a Practice keeps after Consent ends. It is separate from the Client's live profile, vault and unfinished Case work.
_Avoid_: Entire Case archive, live Client record, permanent profile access

**Record custodian**:
A Practice member explicitly entrusted with specified Practice-retained records. This responsibility gives no access to live Client information or authority to sign.
_Avoid_: Practice administrator, Case assignee, owner of the Client

**Dependant authority attestation**:
A Client's statement that they have authority to provide specified information about a dependant for a stated purpose. It does not make the Client that person's Representative or permit consent for another adult's Case.
_Avoid_: Household consent, family account, presumed spousal permission

**Intake**:
A Client's facts supplied for one Case or a chosen solo utility, with a stated purpose for each question. It is neither an immigration application nor a professional conclusion.
_Avoid_: Eligibility application, complete immigration profile

**Language result**:
The reported scores from one attempt at a specified language test, including any identified correction to its report. Scores from different attempts are separate results.
_Avoid_: Overall CLB, best combined scores

**Language conversion**:
The mapping of one language ability's reported test score to an English CLB or French NCLC level or published band under an identified official equivalency chart. It does not establish program eligibility or the result's authenticity.
_Avoid_: Language eligibility, overall language score, French CLB

**Retention hold**:
A documented requirement to preserve specified records while a legal or rights matter is resolved. It does not authorize their use for new Case work.
_Avoid_: Indefinite archive, restored Consent

**Verified representative**:
A Representative whose licence a cadet2 admin has checked against the CICC register or the relevant law society.
_Avoid_: Verified account, platform-endorsed expert

**Representative verification**:
A cadet2 admin's recorded check of a named Representative's identity and professional licence against the responsible regulator. It is separate from Case authority, the review of advice and the verification of a rule or source.
_Avoid_: Email verification, advice approval, source verification

**Escalation**:
Routing of an urgent or sensitive situation to a human Representative instead of automated strategy, including status expiring within 90 days, refusals, removal, criminality, or health concerns.
_Avoid_: Automated emergency advice, eligibility result

**Out of scope**:
Programs and situations cadet2 declines to assess, including Quebec-selected, family, business, humanitarian, and refugee pathways for this pilot.
_Avoid_: Ineligible, blocked, unsupported guess

**Assessment finding**:
A conclusion about an explicitly named program, stage, or criterion on stated facts and dated rules, or an explicit statement that cadet2 cannot assess it. It is not a prediction of an invitation or approval.
_Avoid_: Recommendation rank, eligibility guarantee

**Supported**:
An assessment finding whose stated claim is established by the required facts and applicable, verified rules. Support for one criterion does not establish the whole program.
_Avoid_: Strong fit, high confidence, approved

**Blocked**:
An assessment finding with a demonstrated unmet mandatory criterion under an applicable, verified rule. Missing facts or missing engine coverage do not establish a blocker.
_Avoid_: Probably ineligible, low score, unknown

**Unknown**:
An assessment finding cadet2 cannot establish because required facts, applicable rules, evidence, or assessment capability are missing or unresolved.
_Avoid_: Failed, blocked, out of scope

**Generic pathway**:
A broad planning category, such as study-to-work, that does not identify a particular immigration program and its requirements.
_Avoid_: Named program, supported program coverage

**Calculation receipt**:
The record of an official-rule calculation's inputs, missing facts, rule version, steps, and result, or the explicit reason the calculation is unavailable.
_Avoid_: Fit score, approval probability

**Claim citation**:
A reference connecting one assessment claim to the exact passage of a retained official source and its verification record.
_Avoid_: Related link, source badge

**Verified as of**:
The date a particular claim's use of an official source was checked for meaning and applicability. It does not promise that the source or rules remain unchanged afterward.
_Avoid_: Fresh, current forever, run date

**Client portal**:
The free, mobile-first experience for intake, Signed plans, and non-advice utilities.
_Avoid_: Customer portal, consumer planner

**Representative portal**:
The desktop-first, keyboard-first experience for a Practice's Representatives and Staff to prepare, review, and deliver Case work.
_Avoid_: Agent portal, admin portal

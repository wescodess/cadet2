1. Interview

One question, or one decision, per screen. Every page has the same frame: back, a true save state, the question as the heading, an honest “I don’t know”, a reason on request and one Continue button. An urgent answer interrupts the very next screen with a person to contact.

  • The question is the page heading; nothing competes with it.
  • Each sharing purpose and each acknowledgement gets its own page, so nothing is bundled.
  • An urgent answer replaces the next question with the human route.
  • Check answers is the only place you see everything at once.

Synthetic pilot. Every legal sentence is the registry’s draft text, shown with “Draft wording, pending counsel”. Names, dates and contacts are fictional fixtures.

Best at
Calm, low-effort answering on a phone in short sessions. The fewest decisions per screen of the five, and the clearest separation of consent purposes.
Worst at
Overview. The client can’t see how much is left or jump ahead; about 30 screens from the invitation to the receipt, and the optional parts feel long.
Urgent route
Interrupts immediately on the next screen, full width, with the contact first.
Later moments
Factual replies reuse the same one-question page; withdrawal and account are ordinary pages.

What the pilot does

cadet2 provides software to collect information, organize documents and share a Representative’s Signed plan. The Client portal and its solo tools are free in this pilot. Practices may use the Representative portal to prepare and review work. There is no billing, public directory, matching or lead-selling service in this pilot.

Information and professional advice

cadet2 is not your representative and is not affiliated with the Government of Canada. Its general information and solo tools are not legal advice. Personalized advice is provided by the named Representative in a Signed plan for its stated scope. Ask that person about their advice and any separate service agreement or appointment. Accepting these terms, connecting a Case or signing a plan does not replace a separately required professional agreement or government form. A licence check records an observation; it is not a promise about the advice or an outcome.

Limits

The current engine does not assess named-program eligibility. A supported language conversion or other specific finding establishes only what it says, with the facts, sources and limits shown. Unknown does not mean ineligible. This pilot excludes Quebec-selected, family, business, humanitarian and refugee assessments. Urgent or uncertain safety situations need a Representative’s attention instead of generated strategy. No invitation, approval, processing time or immigration outcome is guaranteed.

Your account and authority

You must be 18 or older to use a portal account. Protect your sign-in details, use your own account and provide information you are authorized to provide. You must have the stated authority before providing identifiable information about a dependant. Do not upload false documents, hide material information, impersonate someone or try to obtain another person’s records. Ask for a correction when information is wrong; do not edit a historical Signed plan.

Choices and records

Terms acceptance does not grant Case sharing or optional utility purposes. Choose each purpose, recipient and exact information separately. You may withdraw Case sharing and continue to use your own permitted tools and Signed plans. The Practice retains only its disclosed Signed plan, material supporting evidence and audit under the approved policy. It does not retain continuing access to your live profile or vault. Access, export, correction and deletion use the Privacy notice’s rights process; a documented retention exception or hold is reported rather than hidden.

Dates, files and notices

Recorded dates are not cadet2’s interpretation of status or legal deadlines. A file upload or scan is not proof that immigration evidence is sufficient. Optional reminders supplement the portal, may be grouped and may fail to arrive. Check your records and contact your Representative for time-sensitive matters. This pilot captures notices without sending them. cadet2 does not provide emergency coverage or promise that a Representative has read a request.

Pilot use and changes

Use supplied fictional Client facts and files only. Do not enter real Client immigration information or rely on a synthetic plan. Participating people still use the normal account and professional-verification controls. Service interruptions or security/authority restrictions may prevent an action; the portal reports actual pending or failed outcomes. Material changes identify a new terms version and request any required new acceptance before the changed use. Past acceptance and signed records keep their original versions. You can decline a changed use and still request your existing rights.

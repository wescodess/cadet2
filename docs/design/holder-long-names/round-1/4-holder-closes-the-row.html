Keeps

  • Any first name reads whole on one line; the full row width is available.
  • Client names, dates and chips get the 82 px the column used, so they wrap less.

Costs

  • Adds a line to every row, but the details get the column’s 82 px back. Measured here: rows average 5% taller at 440 px (133 to 140 px) and 6% shorter at 320 px (202 to 191 px). Queues with short details pay the full extra line, about 28 px.
  • Holders no longer form a column beside the client name; you read down the row ends instead.

Rule change for the owner

  • DESIGN 12.2 Holder: Right-aligned on its own last line of the row; the 24 px disc beside the word
  • DESIGN 12.2 QueueRow: Holder on the last line; rows about 28 px taller

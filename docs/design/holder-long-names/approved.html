Concept 1, refined: Split in place

Keep the 72 px Holder column and the whole row exactly as approved. A name breaks at its spaces first. Only a single word that cannot fit the column on its own line breaks inside the word, and the holder grows downward to fit it.

What it keeps

  • The 72 px Holder column in every row, the disc over the word, the date column, the turn colours and the hidden “Whose turn:” or “Status:” prefix.
  • The 12 px, weight 600 word, never shrunk, hyphenated, abbreviated, clamped or clipped. The full first name stays one piece of text in the page.
  • Discs stay in one straight column, and Client Holder is unchanged.

What it costs

  • A first name wider than 72 px, such as Sivasubramanian, reads as “Sivasubrama / nian”, split with no hyphen. Jean-Baptiste splits at its own hyphen.
  • Ordinary words (You, Client, Unassigned, Signed, Daniel) look exactly as today. A long name adds a line to the holder, and the row grows only if the holder ends up taller than the row’s text.
  • An extreme name grows the row as far as it needs to, with nothing overlapping. Round 1 claimed “zero height change”; that was true only for the names it showed.

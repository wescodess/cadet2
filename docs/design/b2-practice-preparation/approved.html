Triage Desk with Client mirror

Round 1’s Triage Desk, refined with the owner’s decisions of 19 Nov 2026. Views of your own Cases on the left, the queue in the middle and the selected Case beside it. The queue can fold away when the Case needs room, and a question to the Client is previewed exactly as it will land in their B1 Checklist before it is sent.

  • The queue never moves, and it can step aside: folded to a rail, it keeps your row, filters and scroll.
  • Every row and every Case header says who has it next.
  • What the Client will see is shown before it’s sent. Drafts, proposed text and notes never reach the mirror.
  • Nobody grants themselves Representative. The role takes effect only after cadet2’s manual licence check.

Synthetic pilot. Every legal sentence is the registry’s draft text, marked “Draft wording, pending counsel”, with its key. The workforce invitation and the Staff submission statement are placeholders drafted in parallel; their final strings come from the registry. Desk screens are 1440 px; a dashed line marks the bottom of a 1440 by 900 window. Section L also shows 1280 px.

Best at
Speed and continuity: triage, open, act and move on without losing context, keyboard-first, and closest to the approved Relay desk. Now also proves what the Client will see before anything is sent.
Worst at
The mirror needs 360 px, so it sits beside the composer only when the Case pane is at least 760 px: 1440 px with the queue open, or 1280 px with it folded. Otherwise it stacks above Send. Administration still uses the queue shape.
What to watch
Whether Staff find the queue fold and the Facts and Draft tab; whether anyone reads the mirror as the Client’s live screen rather than a preview.

Client mirror beside the composer
Request Ledger’s live preview now sits beside the question composer: the row in the Client’s Tasks and the page they open, at Client sizes, before Send. Drafts, proposed text and notes never appear in it.
1440 px desk and a collapsible queue
Every desk screen is at Relay’s 1440 px design width (Case pane 776 px). The queue folds to a 56 px rail (⌘ \) when the Case needs room; a new Facts and Draft tab pairs each fact with the finding that uses it, side by side when there’s room and stacked when there isn’t.
Representative only after the licence check
An administrator can request the Representative role for themselves; it takes effect only when cadet2 verifies the licence. Request, pending, verified, held, not verified and recheck due are all shown.
Wording drafted, actions on
Workforce invitation create and accept are on, with placeholder text marked “Draft wording, pending counsel”. Staff submission carries a Staff-voice statement that names Daniel and never implies the Client agreed.
Visual decisions applied

Kept from round 1

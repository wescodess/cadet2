2. Request Ledger

The Practice side of the Client’s B1 checklist. A Case is a ledger with the same sections the Client sees, plus a Practice-only section, and every item ends in who holds it. An item opens a focused page and returns to the ledger. Beside it, a live panel shows the Client’s Tasks exactly as they will appear.

  • Same list, both sides: a question you send becomes a task on the Client’s checklist, and you see it land.
  • Every item is a row with a holder, from the Client’s sharing choices to Priya’s review.
  • The Practice-only section (Draft, notes, review) is fenced off and labelled; it never reaches the mirror.
  • Caseload rows list each Case’s open items and who holds each one.

Synthetic pilot. Every legal sentence is the registry’s draft text, marked “Draft wording, pending counsel”, with its registry key. Names, dates, contacts and identifiers are fictional fixtures. The desk is shown at 1280 px; a dashed line marks the bottom of a 1280 by 800 window.

Best at
Coherence with B1 and the hand-off: one pattern for setup, invitations, Cases and the Client’s list; the mirror shows exactly what the Client sees and proves Drafts and notes never leak.
Worst at
Depth of review: going back and forth between ledger and item pages is slow for dense fact checking, Draft findings live a page away from the facts, and the mirror takes 392 px of the 1280 px desk.
What to watch
Whether the mirror reads as the Client’s screen rather than a desk control; whether Representatives want facts and findings on one page.

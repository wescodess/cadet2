5. Fact Grid

Everything is a dense, keyboard-driven grid with an inspector beside it. The caseload is one grid, a Case’s facts are a grid comparing the Client’s answer, the Practice’s entry and each origin, and the Draft is a criterion grid with every input and chart cell. Selecting a row opens its history, receipt or form in the inspector. On narrow screens each row becomes a card.

  • Compare, don’t remember: Client answer, Practice entry, origin and what uses each fact sit in one row.
  • A partial language result is visible at a glance: the speaking row is empty and says why.
  • Arrow keys move through cells and Enter edits; every action also has a visible button.
  • The holder is always the last column.

Synthetic pilot. Every legal sentence is the registry’s draft text, marked “Draft wording, pending counsel”, with its registry key. Names, dates, contacts and identifiers are fictional fixtures. The desk is shown at 1280 px; a dashed line marks the bottom of a 1280 by 800 window.

Best at
Comparison and power use: reported against entered against checked, the four-skill vector with its chart cells, and many Cases scanned at once; fastest for experienced Staff on a keyboard.
Worst at
Accessibility and approachability: an ARIA grid is the hardest pattern to make work with screen readers; the most colours per row; dense for occasional users; narrow and zoomed views need a completely separate card layout.
What to watch
Screen reader testing of grid navigation and cell editing; whether the holder column is still scanned among eight columns.

Setup is a single form with a summary inspector; members are a grid. Workforce invitation wording isn’t registered, so creating and accepting stay off until it is.

B. Invite a Client and work the admitted caseload

Invitations and Cases are grids; the inspector shows the selected row, its turn line and its actions.

C. Prepare facts, submit on purpose and track the human handoff

Daniel edits a cell in the Practice entry column, with origin in the inspector. Submitting shows a changes grid. The handoff is an event grid; Staff see a disabled acknowledgment.

D. Inspect Draft findings and request professional review

Draft 2 is a criterion grid. The speaking row and the four-skill summary say exactly what’s missing. Notes, proposals and questions are one grid with who can see each.

3. Stations

Each Case moves through six named stations: connection and sharing, facts, check and submit, safety and scope, Draft findings, then notes and the review request. A rail shows every station’s state and holder. Each station is one page: the work on the left, and on the right what’s still open there, who holds it and the gate to the next station.

Best atPreventing the wrong step: accidental submission, Staff acknowledging or a review request that hides open items; each blocker names who holds it; the clearest training path for new Staff.

Worst atReal work loops back: a clarification sends a Case back to Facts while a Draft already exists, and the six-column board is cramped at 1280 px; it can be read as a progress bar toward approval.

Signal

People under stress read signs faster than sentences. Signal borrows from airport and transit wayfinding: every state is a sign with its own shape, its own colour and a word, and the shapes mean the same thing on a phone in a queue or on a representative’s board. Type is Atkinson Hyperlegible, drawn so that I, l and 1 never get confused, which matters for licence numbers and for readers whose first language is not English.

  • Shape first. Circle is fine, octagon stops, triangle needs attention, square is moving, rosette is signed, diamond is outside, starburst is urgent.
  • Strong plates, few of them. A state is a solid colour plate with a white sign and a word. Everything else stays white and navy.
  • Legibility is the brand. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next at 18 px for clients, never below 13 px for representatives.
  • Thick lines, no shadows. 2 px borders and a 3 px focus ring with a halo; depth comes from colour fields, not blur.

Specimen data: every name, licence number, case and date here is fictional. “cadet” is a working name.

Foundations

Signal is a wayfinding system. Its foundations are a fixed vocabulary of sign shapes, a navy and white base, and type chosen for legibility before personality.

Typography
One family: Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, from 400 to 800. Heavy weights do the hierarchy; letterforms are built to be told apart.
Colour logic
Navy and white carry the interface. Eight sign colours appear only as solid plates, always with a white sign and a word.
Density
Big for clients (56 px controls, 18 px text). Board-like for representatives: 38 px controls, rows with a 30 px sign column.
Shape
Eight sign shapes are the identity. Containers use 10 to 12 px corners and 2 px borders.
Elevation
None. Depth is a navy colour field or a 2 px outline; overlays get a solid outline, not a blur.
Iconography
Custom sign glyphs: shape for the meaning, pictogram for the state. Lucide at 2.4 px stroke for interface icons.
Motion
Instant. State changes swap in under 100 ms with no easing, like a sign flipping. Only the loading spinner moves.
Focus
A 3 px blue ring, 2 px offset, with a soft halo, visible on navy, white and every plate.

No blur anywhere. A navy field says “this is where to go next”; an outline says “this sits on top”.

Focus

Blue 3 px ring with a 2 px gap and a halo, so it survives on navy fields, colour plates and photographs.

Signs do not animate. Feedback is immediate and literal.

Sign glyphs are solid shapes with a cut-out pictogram. Interface icons are Lucide at a heavier 2.4 px stroke to match the type.

State glyphs

The shape tells you the kind of state before you read the word. The pictogram tells you which one.

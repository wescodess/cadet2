Companion

Immigration is a relationship with a named professional, not a form. Companion is soft, round and warm, and organises colour around one question people ask constantly: whose turn is it? Marigold means you need to act, lake blue means your representative is on it, green means done. Readex Pro, from the Lexend family designed to reduce reading strain, gives the plain-language copy room to breathe and has a path to Arabic and other scripts.

  • Whose turn, always. Marigold is your turn, lake blue is the representative’s, green is done, indigo is signed.
  • People, not tickets. Avatars of the client and the representative appear wherever a decision is made.
  • Soft but exact. Round shapes and tinted surfaces, with the same strict state vocabulary as every other option.
  • Elevation is warmth. Tinted shadows lift what you can act on; everything else rests on the page.

Specimen data: every name, licence number, case and date here is fictional. “cadet” is a working name.

Foundations

Companion is built around the relationship between a client and their representative. Its foundations are round shapes, tinted surfaces, warm tinted elevation, and a colour logic that encodes whose turn it is.

Typography
Readex Pro, from the Lexend family, at 350 to 600. Generous widths and open shapes for readers working in a second language.
Colour logic
Colour answers “whose turn?”: marigold for the client, lake blue for the representative, green for done, indigo for signed.
Density
Comfortable. Client controls are 52 px pills; representative rows are 34 px controls with roomy 12 px row padding.
Shape
Round. Pill controls and chips, 22 px client cards, 16 px representative cards, circular avatars everywhere.
Elevation
Two tinted shadows: a low lift for things you can act on, a deep one for overlays. Pages rest on a green-grey ground.
Iconography
Lucide icons set inside solid colour discs, so every state reads as a small round token with a picture.
Motion
Gentle springs on confirmation (220 ms, slight overshoot): a chip settling, a card arriving. Off with reduced motion.
Focus
A 3 px lake-blue ring with a 3 px gap that follows the pill shape.

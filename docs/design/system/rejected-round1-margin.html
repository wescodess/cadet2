Margin

Every plan in cadet is a record a licensed person stands behind. Margin treats it that way: calm reading type, ink on paper, and a consistent margin where the reviewer’s marks live: ticks, crosses, queries and carets, each in its own ink. Status is something written on the record, not a badge stuck on top of it.

  • Read like a letter, check like a ledger. Literata carries the words people must understand; Schibsted Grotesk carries the controls.
  • Colour is ink. Each state has its own ink colour, a highlighter swipe and a reviewer’s mark. The only solid fill in the system is the urgent stamp.
  • The margin is sacred. Marks, dates and provenance sit in a fixed left margin so the eye learns where to look.
  • No shadows on paper. Sheets are separated by tone and rules; only dialogs and toasts lift off the page.

Specimen data: every name, licence number, case and date here is fictional. “cadet” is a working name.

Foundations

Margin is built from paper, ink and a reviewer’s marks. The same tokens drive both portals; the client expression reads like a letter, the representative expression reads like an annotated brief.

Typography
Literata, a screen-reading serif, for headings and anything a person must understand. Schibsted Grotesk for controls, labels and tables.
Colour logic
Colour is ink. Eight state inks on cool paper; ink navy for actions. The urgent stamp is the only solid fill.
Density
Typographic, not boxy. Client text at 18 px on generous leading; representative at 14 px with 15 px serif for draft text.
Shape
4 px corners on controls. Sheets are ruled, not boxed. A fixed left margin column holds marks and dates.
Elevation
Flat paper. Tone and rules separate layers; only dialogs and toasts cast a shadow.
Iconography
Custom reviewer’s marks for states: tick, cross, query, caret, strike, signature. Lucide for everything else.
Motion
Ink, not animation: colour and opacity fades at 160 ms. Nothing slides, nothing bounces.
Focus
A 3 px ultramarine outline, 2 px offset, on every control in both themes.

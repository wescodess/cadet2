# Separate Practice-retained records from live Client sharing

Seal a minimal, self-contained Signed plan and its material signing/disclosure/custody evidence separately from Client-owned profile/vault data and unfinished Case work. Per-Case withdrawal stops live authority immediately; retained read/export requires its own record-specific grant and purpose, and account deletion removes everything outside that boundary except documented holds and minimal recovery/rights receipts. Retention text, legal bases, file sufficiency and durations remain pending counsel; synthetic staging does not authorize real-data use.

Deleting the entire file would destroy the Practice's settled signed/audit record, while retaining the whole Case would defeat the Client's settled deletion rights and expose live information after withdrawal. Independent sealed evidence accepts extra manifests, access checks and deletion reconciliation so the signed record survives without live profile or vault joins. Changing this boundary after signatures exist would require a material migration of records, disclosures and rights obligations; retaining personal evidence after account deletion must not become an accidental cascade exception.

See [Per-Case consent, retention and data rights](../decisions/consent-retention-and-data-rights.md) for the retained set, current-authority policy, cross-store deletion/restore protocol, pending-counsel schedule and first proof seams. This extends ADR 0003's Retained branch and the system-of-record/assessment contracts; it approves no account, spending, migration, data deletion, deployment or production activation.

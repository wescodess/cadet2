# Bind legal acknowledgements to immutable text versions

For the synthetic staging-pilot specification, legal notices, purpose choices and signing attestations come from one immutable versioned registry; acknowledgement and signature receipts preserve the exact rendered text, version, scope and pending-counsel/synthetic status. This rejects scattered inline copy and mutable latest-policy links because later edits must not change what a Client accepted or what a Representative signed; rebuilding that history after records exist would be costly and unreliable. The cost is explicit version publication and affected reacceptance/re-consent, with historical copies kept under the approved retention policy; legal wording and effect remain **pending counsel**, and real Client data remains blocked as specified in [Legal-floor content and pending-counsel boundaries](../decisions/legal-floor-content.md).

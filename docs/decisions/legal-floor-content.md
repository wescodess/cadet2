## Registry and acknowledgement contract

The inventory below is the first registry specification, **not a registry implemented by this PR**. Each full identifier `lf.<key>.v1.en` denotes one immutable English text version. Every row is **pending counsel**, including short labels, state messages, the two signing texts, and the longer terms/privacy drafts. Alternatives in a row are named variants in that version; each rendered receipt binds the selected variant. A wording or meaning change creates a new version. Counsel approval cannot silently change text or retroactively turn synthetic acceptance into real-data consent.

Each registry entry must carry its identifier, exact text/variants and digest, locale, purpose, permitted surfaces, acknowledgement kind, typed interpolation schema, dependencies, pending-counsel status, synthetic-use restriction and revision history. Its status also records approval/withdrawal evidence: reviewer, scope/jurisdictions, decision date, approved text digest, effective dates and policy dependencies. A code merge, admin checkbox, owner UI approval or passing test is not counsel approval. A source's verified-as-of is never derived from that approval date.

Placeholders such as `{practice}`, `{recipients}`, `{selected_facts}` and `{scope}` require server-resolved, authorized, exact values. The person must see the selected fields/categories, named people and roles, document versions, actions, relevant sensitive data, retention consequences and processor disclosure before assent. An omitted placeholder, unconfigured contact or unknown text version blocks the dependent action; it never renders a reassuring default. Escape values as text. Public source-check attribution uses an approved person/process label, never a private licence verifier's identity or internal security identifier. Free-form legal disclaimers, recipient selectors such as “all future Staff”, and arbitrary regulator URLs cannot enter through interpolation.

| Record code | How use or acceptance is recorded |
| --- | --- |
| **D — disclosure** | No checkbox or inferred acceptance. Bind text ID/digest and contextual evidence to the page/content revision, plan edition/export manifest or relevant action receipt. Anonymous reading needs no new tracking log. Disclosure is not Consent or a waiver. |
| **A — acknowledgement** | Explicit unchecked terms acceptance or notice acknowledgement by the authenticated person. Record the exact acknowledged text and action separately; noticing a privacy policy is not agreement to unrelated processing. |
| **C — purpose choice** | Explicit unchecked opt-in for that feature or Case purpose, with its own current grant/revision and scope. Record decline as decline when submitted; unasked/unanswered is no grant. Terms acceptance and connection acceptance supply no purpose. |
| **T — attestation** | An explicit personal assertion, bound to its subject and scope/revision. Record accepted/declined/missing/disputed/retracted distinctly. Staff cannot attest for the Client. |
| **S — signing** | Both separate attestation versions, rendered texts and accepted values are sealed in the same Plan signature receipt for one exact edition and signing intent. Missing/refused either text denies signing. |
| **R — rights/action receipt** | Record the requested scope and actual committed outcome of withdrawal, deletion, submission, connection or export. Confirmation is not a new consent grant, release of rights or promise that remote copies were erased. |
| **M — message** | Bind template version and rendered content digest to the outbox/capture receipt plus current purpose/channel authority. No acceptance is inferred from sending, opening or clicking. Capture is explicitly not sent. |

For every A/C/T/S record preserve actor derived from the live session, subject/Case/Practice or utility as applicable, server UTC time, text ID/version/locale, exact rendered text and digest, structured selected scope and canonical digest, dependency versions, explicit action/outcome, expected authority revision, environment/synthetic status, operation key and audit reference. Link the applicable terms/privacy/retention/processor versions. Do not collect an IP address, fingerprint, identity document or birth date solely to prove a checkbox. Apply the consent decision's minimal retained evidence and approved schedule rather than keeping every intermediate render forever.

The authoritative Convex mutation validates the exact server-issued text bundle, actor, versions and scopes, then commits receipt, effective grant/action and audit atomically. Identical operation-key retries return the same committed result; changed payloads conflict. A timeout is pending/outcome unknown until reconciled. A caller-supplied text string, `accepted: true`, client clock, route visit or old receipt cannot create authority. Direct Convex, Nitro, workers, export and notification paths consume the same current authority.

Text status and a person's acceptance are separate. Text states include pending counsel, approved for a stated use, superseded and withdrawn. Consent states retain proposed/no grant, active, superseded, withdrawn and expired; UI also distinguishes declined, missing and version change requiring a new choice. Material changes to purpose, data, recipients, actions or legal terms require the affected fresh acknowledgement/Consent before that changed use. Narrowing authority applies immediately. Old receipts and signed bytes remain historical; no silent migration, backdating or replay of jobs. The Client can still withdraw, inspect status and request rights when a new version is declined or a policy service is unavailable.

## Glossary additions

`GLOSSARY.md` is intentionally unchanged. Proposed additions for the orchestrator to reconcile:

- **Legal text version:** One fixed wording of a notice, term, consent statement or attestation, preserved so people can see what applied to their action. Avoid: latest-policy link, generic disclaimer.
- **Pending counsel:** Draft legal wording or policy awaiting the required professional legal review; it permits only the expressly bounded synthetic use. Avoid: approved, legally safe, temporary legal clearance.
- **Acknowledgement:** A person's recorded recognition or acceptance of the specified terms or notice. It does not itself grant a separate information-use purpose. Avoid: blanket Consent, page view.

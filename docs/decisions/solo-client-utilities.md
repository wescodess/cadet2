# Solo Client utilities and reminders

Decision date: 2026-11-17. **answered-on-auto** for utility behavior in the private staging-pilot specification; **owner provider/account questions remain open** below. Answers [Define solo Client utilities and reminder behavior](https://github.com/wescodess/cadet2/issues/41) under [Chart the Client and Representative portals for a staging pilot](https://github.com/wescodess/cadet2/issues/29). This record specifies behavior and content, implements no feature, and approves no visual design, paid provider, account, deployment or real Client data.

**A solo Client can record dates, receive optional generic reminders, organize and privately store documents, read sourced general information, and accept a known Practice's invitation.** These utilities never determine what the person qualifies for, their status rights, a legal deadline, or what immigration action they should take. Personalized output reaches the Client only in a Signed plan. Utility data belongs to the Client without a Case or a fabricated Practice.

Branch `decide/solo-client-utilities` is based on **`origin/decide/consent-and-data-rights` at `43efdb14d27c1f616d1cb4a18639dc5e097e79b0`**, rather than directly on `dev`. Its application baseline is `dev` at `fe2b4a4e4f8b4b861fb5c92661b9e411a84ef0b3`; the PR targets `dev` and includes unmerged prerequisite documentation. Only this record and the newly resolved glossary terms are this decision's delta. No additional ADR: reminder defaults and utility mechanics are reversible specification choices; the consequential authority/retention boundaries already have ADRs 0003 and 0004. No new choice meets all three ADR criteria.

## The non-advice boundary

Recording a person's supplied fact and doing calendar arithmetic is permitted; selecting or interpreting a legal rule for that person is personalized assessment/advice. A disclaimer cannot turn the latter into a utility. All examples below are behavioral copy examples; legal wording remains **pending counsel** under [Define legal-floor content and pending-counsel boundaries](https://github.com/wescodess/cadet2/issues/47).

| Utility | May say/do | Must not say/do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Date tracker | “You entered 18 December as the expiry date. That date is 7 calendar days away.” Show who supplied it and any source. “The date you entered has passed” describes the record. | “You lose status on 18 December”; “Apply for an extension by 18 November”; infer maintained status, a filing deadline, restoration period, work permission, test validity or a safe time to wait. |
| Reminder | In the authenticated portal: “A date you saved is approaching.” External email: only the generic template below. | Tell the person what immigration step to take, embed the date/title/status in email, or label an overdue personal task a missed legal deadline. |
| Checklist/vault | “Added by you”; “Copied from this official checklist”; “File uploaded; scan pending”; “Not yet reviewed for this Case.” Record a Client's own completion choice. | “You need these documents because you qualify”; “Your proof is valid”; “Application ready”; completeness percentages implying sufficiency or approval. Scan-clean is not evidence acceptance or authenticity. |
| General information | “This page summarizes the linked official information, checked on this date. We have not assessed your circumstances.” Let readers choose a topic. | “This program fits you”; tailored requirements, rankings, approval odds, strategy, profile-based filters or an eligibility badge. |
| Connect to a Representative | Accept/decline a received invitation; identify its Practice and intended recipients; link the CICC register for independent checking. | Search a cadet2 directory, rank professionals, request a referral from cadet2, broadcast a lead, sell contact details, book/pay for a matched adviser or imply endorsement. |

A Client may type a private label such as “appeal date” or copy advice they already received. Attribute it as **entered by you**, escape it as text, and never restate it as cadet2's instruction. No automatic classification of free text, OCR, document extraction or portal LLM. Do not require a full immigration profile to use a date or store a file.

For connected Clients, distinguish **clerical coordination** from **personalized direction**. A consented Case may request an existing document, acknowledge receipt, state who is working, or confirm a transcription. A requirement selected for that Client, interpreted deadline, instruction to file/renew/stop working, or case-specific strategy is disclosed only as part of an exact Signed plan. A Case task carrying that direction must reference that signed version and reproduce its meaning; editing it materially requires new review/signature. Staff cannot create unsigned advice through a checklist or reminder. A professional's confirmation of a date records authorship and source; it is not cadet2's legal validation.

## Date tracker contract

### Kinds, provenance and ownership

These are the complete initial kinds. All accept **known date** or **unknown**; no empty string, zero timestamp, assumed year, inferred date or silent “not applicable” default represents unknown.

| Kind | Source of the value; allowed meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Status/permit document expiry | Client manually enters the date shown on their document or reports an unknown date. An assigned Representative may confirm that transcription in a consented Case. The field does not certify the person's actual status. |
| Passport/identity document expiry | Client enters the printed expiry; optional exact vault version reference. No prediction of renewal time or travel/immigration validity. |
| Test/evidence expiry | Client enters an explicit expiry stated in a report/notice or supplied to them. A test-taking date may instead be a personal reference date; the app never adds a presumed validity period. CLB conversion does not calculate this expiry. |
| Notified deadline | Client transcribes a deadline from correspondence, with optional notice/version reference. Do not derive it by adding days to a refusal, service or receipt date. A personalized interpretation supplied by the Representative requires a Signed plan binding. |
| Personal target/reference date | Client's own target, such as “organize my papers”, plainly labelled personal. No legal consequence is inferred from passing it. |
| Appointment | Client enters a known appointment date/time and source. No booking, attendee invitation or calendar integration is implied. |
| Representative task | Case-only, attributed to the named professional, with responsible person and exact source. Administrative requests can reference the recorded request; personalized actions require the Signed plan binding above. |

Store an opaque ID, Client owner, kind, private label, optional dependant reference, known/unknown value, date precision, source category, source reference/version, supplied-by identity/time, confirmation record if any, responsible person, lifecycle and revisions. Never store a document's contents in the label. Source category is **Client-entered** or **Representative-confirmed**; record the underlying document/notice or Signed plan separately. No source is inferred merely because someone uploaded a file. Missing source detail is disclosed, not fabricated.

A solo date is Client-owned with no Case. Sharing selected date fields into a Case creates a separately consented snapshot and Case reminder scope. A Representative edits/confirms that Case copy only; they cannot alter the global solo date. Staff may prepare a proposed Case date internally; it becomes a Client-visible tracked date only through explicit Client entry or Representative confirmation, with the signed-only rule still applying to personalized direction. A Client correction does not silently update the Case. Confirmation is tied to exact value/source revisions, confirmer, Case and time; an edit removes current confirmation until rechecked. Preserve prior attribution as history. A removed Representative's confirmation is historical, never a continuing assignment or a new licence claim.

Lifecycle: **active**, **completed**, **archived**, **deleted**. Known/unknown and confirmation are separate dimensions. Passing a date does not complete or archive it. “Completed” means the user marked the organizational task done, not that IRCC received/approved anything or status is protected. Reopening or copying an item requires an explicit action and creates future work only. Deletion first denies access and schedules rights cleanup.

### Calendar, time and recurrence

- Store a date-only value as a validated Gregorian `YYYY-MM-DD`, never UTC midnight. Optional timed appointments/deadlines store local date/time, explicit IANA zone, selected offset and resolved UTC instant. Validate real dates at the server, including leap years. Display precision/source; missing time does not mean a midnight legal cutoff.
- Require a confirmed IANA **reminder timezone** before scheduling. Suggest the browser's zone but require acceptance; do not assume Toronto, the Representative's zone or a legal jurisdiction. Persist the zone per schedule. Travel/browser changes do not change it. An explicit account-zone change asks which schedules to update; changed schedules get a new revision and a preview of future reminder times.
- Default delivery time is **09:00** in that schedule's zone. Lead times subtract calendar days from the recorded date, not fixed 24-hour blocks. Resolve with current timezone data, saving the calculation/version. For a nonexistent local reminder time use the first valid instant after the gap; for a repeated reminder time use its earlier occurrence, once. If an entire calendar day is skipped, mark that occurrence unavailable rather than notify on a different date. An ambiguous/nonexistent user-entered timed deadline is unresolved until the Client explicitly selects an offset or corrects it; do not silently choose its legal meaning.
- All tracked dates are **one-off**. The four lead-time reminders below are occurrences for that one date, not a recurring deadline. No annual passport/permit renewal, rolling status calculation, monthly repeating tasks or automatic successor date. A replacement date is deliberately entered as a new revision/item.
- The server clock determines due work and calendar-day distance. A date-only “today/passed” comparison uses the saved zone as an organizational convention, not a ruling on when rights expire. DTOs carry server as-of/timezone/revision; the Client refreshes at local day boundaries and after reconnect. Device-clock changes cannot advance sends. Persisted due-work processing advances portal notices even when no query is open.
- Unknown dates remain visible with “Date not known; no date reminders scheduled” and a clarification action. Never calculate urgency or safety from a missing date. Conflicting Client/Case values show both sources and “needs clarification”; the app chooses neither as the legally correct date.

If the **entered status/permit expiry** is within 90 calendar days, is today, or has passed, retain a human-help notice regardless of email preferences or task completion. Say that the recorded date needs professional attention, not that status has ended. Unknown status expiry says urgency cannot be determined. For an active consented Case, offer its named Representative contact route and a receipt only when a request actually commits. Otherwise offer the independent CICC register and “ask a representative you know for an invitation”; no matching service or automatic sharing. Never promise response time, monitoring or emergency coverage. Known refusal/removal/criminality/health concerns use the same non-strategy human path from the intake contract; the tracker adds no narrative collection/classifier. Detailed intake triage remains with [Define intake content, scope detection and urgent escalation](https://github.com/wescodess/cadet2/issues/42).

## Reminders, suppression and delivery truth

### Schedule and choices

Saving a date always preserves it in the portal. **Reminder scheduling is a separate express choice per date**; email additionally requires an unchecked, account-level email opt-in, a verified current address and the approved transport. Both solo and Case choices are optional. Practice members cannot turn on a Client's email, change their address/preferences or select a different recipient. Case scheduling also needs current `case.reminders` for the exact dates and named assignees. Logging out does not cancel independently authorized durable schedules.

The initial schedule is **30, 7, 1 and 0 calendar days before the recorded date at 09:00** in the saved zone. The Client may remove any offsets or disable the schedule; custom offsets, snooze, SMS, push, WhatsApp and recurring chasers are deferred. On enable/create/change/reopen, schedule only occurrences strictly after that commit and before the recorded timed target (or within its date-only calendar day). Do not backfill missed offsets, send immediately because an old date was entered, or send a due-today reminder after a timed target has passed. The portal immediately shows the date and any urgency; email is supplementary.

At each occurrence, create one durable portal reminder referencing the current item. Reading/marking a notice read is not task completion and does not remove the date or human-help notice. Group external routine reminders for a Client: **at most one accepted or possibly accepted generic utility-notice envelope in any rolling 24 hours**. Due occurrences ready in the same dispatch batch share that envelope. Later occurrences inside that window stay visible in the portal and record `portal_only_rate_limited`; they are not deferred into a burst. Explain this limit in notification preferences. This limit does not apply to separately bounded verification/reset traffic and is not a delivery guarantee per date.

Due work may recover only while `now < min(dueAt + 24 hours, next selected occurrence, timed target or end of the date-only day)`. The last bound is a notification-staleness cutoff, never a legal cutoff. Older occurrences become **skipped_late**. Unknown/unresolved dates create no occurrence. Finite retries stop at the same bound. A 5-minute indexed, bounded recovery sweep supplements scheduled wake-ups; pagination/leases and oldest-due ordering prevent an unbounded scan. Missed email never removes the underlying date.

### Cancellation and suppression

Recheck current authority at portal publication and the last authoritative dispatch admission before an external effect. Commit denial/revision and audit/work cancellation atomically; cancelling a scheduler handle alone is insufficient.

| Trigger | Required outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Date/time/zone/offset change or conflict/unknown | Fence old pending occurrences/envelopes; calculate only future occurrences for the new revision. A label-only edit does not restart sent offsets. Keep old receipts historical. |
| Complete/archive/delete item or withdraw its utility purpose | Cancel dependent future work. Deletion additionally invalidates portal payloads and starts cleanup. Reopening is explicit and does not replay old mail. |
| Email opt-out, unverified/changed address | Stop email immediately. Re-verification and a fresh affirmative preference apply to the new address; do not reroute queued messages. Portal dates remain. Old-address delivery suppression is not silently cleared. |
| Hard bounce or complaint | Suppress that address/channel across cadet2 sends subject to the approved auth/recovery contract. Correct/verify an address and explicitly opt in again, with supported provider suppression remediation before sending. Resend cannot bypass a complaint. Keep only the bounded suppression evidence justified by the rights policy. |
| Temporary provider rejection/outage/quota | Retry only known non-acceptance within the lateness bound; show retry/failure separately. After the initial call, at most three retries at 1, 5 and 30 minutes after the preceding failure. Honor a longer provider retry-after only within the bound. Permanent rejection fails immediately. |
| Case Consent ends, selected recipient loses authority, Case is suspended/closed or source Signed plan is superseded/restricted | Cancel dependent Case occurrences, including grouped-envelope members. Historical Client-own retrieval remains subject to rights; an old Case task cannot keep issuing directions. Independent solo schedules continue. No copying Case data into solo scope as an automatic workaround. |
| Principal/account disable/deletion, dependant authority withdrawn, feature/region gate closes | Deny the affected work immediately; fence callbacks/late workers and clean it through the rights manifest. Lost session alone is not account deletion. Scope a dependant restriction to affected records. |

A changed Consent revision fences old Case work even when some scope remains; continued scheduling requires a fresh explicit action against the new revision. Re-consent, rejoining a Practice, provider activation and backup restore never replay cancelled or captured notices. Suppression does not prevent the Client cancelling/deleting a reminder or reading their own permitted dates.

### Outbox and idempotency

1. A guarded Convex mutation resolves the Client/Case from stored authority, checks expected revisions and commits the schedule, required audit, uniquely keyed occurrences and work intent together. A principal/action-scoped operation key with a canonical input digest returns the original receipt on an identical retry; changed input with that key conflicts. Scheduled arguments contain opaque work references, not copied dates, titles or recipients.
2. A logical occurrence key binds **environment, reminder ID, schedule revision, offset, resolved UTC time and channel**. Transactional uniqueness prevents duplicate wake-ups/sweeps creating another occurrence. Previously emitted offsets are not regenerated by metadata edits. Envelope membership binds exact occurrence/authority revisions; prune newly denied members before dispatch, and cancel an empty envelope. Never expose another Client's items through grouping.
3. Atomically claim an attempt with a lease/fence and reserve the Client's rolling notice slot plus environment capacity. Resolve the current verified recipient and template server-side. Recheck active principal, utility/channel choices, suppression, schedule/source, applicable Case/member/Consent/dependant authority and environment gates. Workers use the recorded business intent, never an old user JWT or deployment-key impersonation.
4. Commit **dispatching** before the transport call. A stale attempt cannot finalize or begin a new dispatch; lease expiry before dispatch permits a fenced retry. Once dispatch may have started, lease expiry/crash/timeout/lost receipt is **send_unknown**, even if it later turns out no call occurred. Preserve the rolling slot because acceptance is possible. Do not automatically resend unknown messages. Reconcile authenticated provider events/records against the exact envelope/attempt; no new key or manual resend shortcut. Only evidence of non-acceptance permits the bounded retry. Provider acceptance with no delivery event remains acceptance, not a reason to resend.
5. A callback authenticates the actual provider mechanism, checks freshness/replay and environment, deduplicates its event ID and correlates the registered message/attempt. An opaque correlation token may be registered before dispatch to reconcile a lost provider response; it is not a Case ID. Store bounded event facts with monotonic outcomes: delivered cannot regress to queued; a later complaint can still add suppression. A callback cannot reactivate cancelled work, reveal a deleted item or create Consent.

The permission recheck and external send cannot be one transaction. Cancellation committed before dispatch admission prevents the send; an effect admitted earlier may already be in flight and cannot be recalled. Record that distinction. This specifies one logical occurrence and conservative ambiguity handling, **not exactly-once external email**. [Convex scheduling documentation](https://docs.convex.dev/scheduling/scheduled-functions), rechecked 2026-11-17, supports mutation-atomic scheduling, but actions are not automatically retried and scheduled auth is not inherited. Durable business receipts and application reconciliation remain necessary.

Show separate schedule and delivery states: **off**, **scheduled**, **cancelled**, **skipped** with reason; **captured_synthetic**, **dispatching**, **provider_accepted**, **delivered_to_mail_server**, **retrying**, **failed**, **suppressed**, **send_unknown**. Delivery is not reading, task completion or an immigration outcome. A callback received after cancellation can annotate an already admitted send's historical outcome without reopening the schedule. Keep portal activity and current task/date status separate.

### External content and synthetic capture

The reminder template is fixed: subject **“A reminder is available in cadet2”**; body **“Sign in to view your reminders.”** Link only to the fixed configured authenticated reminders route. No bearer token, record IDs or personal query parameters. Exclude names, dates, titles/free text, status/permit types, nationality, UCI/passport/application numbers, dependants, Case/Practice/Representative labels, medical/criminal/refusal facts, assessment/plan content, documents and attachments from subject/body/headers/tags/callback URLs. Disable open/click tracking. Minimize provider errors and receipts; no private payload logs. The address and platform relationship are still identifying data. Recipient mailbox geography/forwarding is outside cadet2's storage-region control; disclose this limitation without claiming Canadian-only mailbox delivery.

Initial transport is **synthetic capture only**, in the environment's verified Canadian boundary (or an isolated local synthetic harness). Enforce an exact server-owned synthetic recipient allowlist, one recipient, no CC/BCC/recipient overrides, and environment isolation. Reject real addresses supplied through profile changes, invites, reset requests and replayed jobs. Missing/malformed configuration denies; never fall back to SMTP, a non-Canadian service or the live provider when capture fails. Deployed staging contains no test/seed/mock endpoints; trusted fixture setup and test doubles belong to the isolated harness.

Capture uses the same admission, outbox, suppression, idempotency and content rules, ending in **captured_synthetic**, explicitly “not sent”. Its environment-isolated records live in Convex; persist a unique envelope receipt atomically and consume the synthetic notice budget so duplicate attempts and rolling limits are exercised. No live sender credentials exist in the capture worker. Captured notices are terminal and never flushed to live mail on activation. An authenticated Client can inspect only their permitted reminder history; an operator gets minimized failure counters, not a general capture inbox. Any necessary auth/invite token retrieval uses a separate short-lived, narrowly authorized test receipt, redacted from generic captures/logs; it is not a public mailbox or deployed bypass. Auth and invitation templates are separate from utility notices and retain the tenancy contract's token rules.

Local capture/state tests prove neither Canadian hosted execution nor real DNS/inbox delivery. Provider simulation, approved controlled-mailbox delivery and staging QA are separate evidence. Reserve auth/recovery capacity ahead of reminders, enforce per-principal abuse controls and environment-wide send/storage/scan budgets before admission; numeric funded environment caps come from the owner checklist. A cap exhaustion is visible and fails closed rather than creating paid overage work silently.

Specific personalized conclusions remain in the Signed plan. A new file version begins unreviewed and cannot inherit an older version's acceptance. Private Staff notes/Drafts stay outside Client projections. No public/private pipeline may read the vault to generate a checklist.

### Upload envelope and state machine

Adopt the researched conservative envelope as **reversible synthetic-pilot engineering limits**, not provider maxima or a future paid entitlement: **PDF, JPEG and PNG; 1 byte–10 MiB per file; 25 stored file versions and 250 MiB per Client; two concurrent upload reservations**. Versions, quarantine, partials and reservations count; replacement does not bypass limits. Quota is reserved transactionally in Convex before bytes move and reconciled against actual stored versions. Deletion releases stored-byte quota only after confirmed purge; backup/retained exceptions remain separately budgeted, never labelled erased. Environment-wide storage/scan/retry caps additionally gate writes.

Use bounded authenticated Canadian upload/download execution with private object keys. Enforce actual streamed byte count, content signature/extension/type consistency, parser validity and final checksum/size; browser MIME/`accept` is no proof. Reject archives, Office/executable files, HTML/SVG, encrypted/password-protected PDFs, active scripts/embedded attachments, malformed or uninspectable content. Bound parser/scan CPU, memory and time; no OCR, AI extraction, public malware-analysis service or Client-controlled “clean” flag. Store an escaped original display name only in protected metadata, not bucket keys/tags/logs. No cross-Client deduplication or existence hints.

| State | Meaning and permitted next action |
| --- | --- |
| **reserved / uploading** | Client ownership/purpose and quota admitted; immutable upload/version ID, attempt fence and a 30-minute reservation. Bytes go only to private quarantine. Cancel/timeout denies completion and creates partial/orphan cleanup. Retrying a lost admission returns the same receipt; a new upload attempt gets a new fenced object key. |
| **validating / scan_pending** | Server completion binds exact object version, actual size/type/checksum and still-current ownership. Technical validation and a trusted scanner are required; neither Client nor Practice may download/share the bytes yet. Scan pending for 30 minutes becomes a visible scan failure requiring reconciliation/retry, not clean. |
| **available** | Technical checks and a trusted **clean** result for that exact immutable version pass; finalization rechecks ownership, fences and configuration. It may be downloaded by its Client owner and explicitly shared. “Available” says nothing about evidence adequacy. |
| **rejected / quarantined / scan_failed** | Respectively unsupported/invalid input, detected threat, or missing/unsupported/failed/denied/timed-out scan. No downloads, exports or Case sharing of bytes. Show the safe reason and replace/retry option; do not expose malware samples or a scan bypass. Conflicting scan receipts restrict the file until reconciled. |
| **upload_failed / unavailable** | Interrupted transfer, missing/mismatched object, storage/scanner outage or disabled configuration. Preserve a receipt and next action; never claim upload/scan success. Reconcile a possible provider success before retrying. |
| **deleting / deletion_retrying / deleted** | Immediate metadata denial and durable cleanup; retain pending/retry status until all live versions/partials/derivatives are verified removed. Recovery-copy expiry and retained/held exceptions remain separate. |

Rejected, cancelled and orphaned uploads are due for purge within **24 hours** in the synthetic configuration; failures stay inaccessible and visibly overdue/retrying. These finite engineering clocks do not set statutory retention. Keep cleanup intents even if quota/admission metadata expires. A late scan/upload event cannot publish a deleted/cancelled version or recreate a Case share. Authenticated service callbacks bind environment, upload/object version, checksum mapping and attempt; duplicates are idempotent. A scan retry needs an explicit new attempt, not reuse of a stale result. Store a finite scan-policy/version record; scanner health/signature currency must be verified before availability.

No automatic duplicate deletion, replacement overwrite or hidden conversion. A new version gets a new scan/review/share state. Downloads use attachment disposition and the verified media type, with no active document execution. Preview/thumbnail/extraction services are deferred; adding one would extend the Canadian processor and deletion inventory. A missing approved store/scanner blocks byte upload; it does not activate the RAG adapter/local fallback. Isolated fixture tests can prove states, not pretend scanning exists on hosted staging.

### Access, sharing, withdrawal and deletion

The Client can manage their own permitted files without a Case, subject to current session/principal and 18+/dependant authority. All metadata/list/count/search/export paths use that same authority; no entire-vault Practice view, admin bypass, public bucket or share-by-URL. Downloads stream through the authenticated Canadian path, resolving the exact stored version and current scan/lifecycle status. Recheck every new request, Range and retry; private/no-store responses, no durable offline copy or browser-visible provider bearer URL. Already admitted/downloaded bytes cannot be recalled.

To share, the Client deliberately selects an **available exact version**, Case, named recipients, purpose and view/download actions under `case.documents` and its applicable live scopes. Display the selection and retention boundary before assent. Commit the share receipt with current Consent and audit, or deny all of it. Folder sharing, accepting an invite, linking an unavailable file, uploading a replacement and granting another Case never share it automatically. A newer version requires new explicit selection; Case A's share cannot authorize Case B at the same Practice. A document's availability is not Consent.

Unsharing/withdrawing denies dependent metadata/bytes/exports and work at its authority commit, clears stale portal state and fences late callbacks. The solo original remains available to the Client. Account/resource deletion instead follows the [consent/rights manifest](consent-retention-and-data-rights.md): deny first; invalidate every live share and export depending on that version; purge current/noncurrent versions, quarantine/partials, scanner scratch, derivatives and renditions; reconcile actual provider receipts/listing. A delete marker, removed DB row or latest-version 404 is not version erasure. Do not delete independently supplied records belonging to someone else.

Raw vault bytes are **not Practice-retained by default**, even when reviewed. A Signed plan retains only the sealed material evidence projection and source version/checksum specified by the rights decision; a historical reference grants no live file download. Valid scoped holds and counsel-approved exceptions are disclosed separately. Exports include the owned manifest/available originals through the same current checks; quarantined/missing/restricted items are explicitly unavailable. Export/deletion covers checklist revisions, dates/reminder receipts/captures and identifying delivery metadata too. Report live erasure, exceptions and `backup_expiry_pending` separately. Restore stays closed until the independent Canadian deletion/revocation journal is replayed and complete; no document, share or reminder may resurrect.

# Representative verification and signature evidence

Decision date: 2026-11-17. Status: **answered-on-auto** for the private design-partner pilot specification. Resolves [Decide Representative verification and signature evidence](https://github.com/wescodess/cadet2/issues/37) under [Chart the Client and Representative portals for a staging pilot](https://github.com/wescodess/cadet2/issues/29). This record specifies mechanisms; signing, regulatory compliance and staging readiness remain unproved.

**Only a currently admin-verified, assigned Representative can interactively sign one exact Client edition for one consented Case.** The Plan signature seals the content, assessment/input/edit revisions, relied-on evidence, Consent and signer identity at the server's signing time. Later credential changes add current warnings and restrict new work; they never rewrite the historical signature. Corrections require a new revision, review and signature.

This branch starts from `origin/decide/consent-and-data-rights` at `43efdb14d27c1f616d1cb4a18639dc5e097e79b0`, preserving its glossary and ADR 0004. The code baseline remains `dev` at `fe2b4a4e4f8b4b861fb5c92661b9e411a84ef0b3`. No new ADR is warranted: immutable assessment/signing evidence and the independent retained boundary are already decided in ADRs 0001 and 0004; the new verification cadence and ceremony are versioned, revisable controls without a new surprising architectural trade-off. The glossary adds only **Representative verification**, **Plan signature** and **Superseding plan**.

## Verification operation and recorded evidence

Add only a pending/expiring/held verification queue, credential/evidence detail and reasoned approve/reject/hold/recheck actions to the existing cadet2 admin area. The operation needs no Client/Case content, advice preview or general Case browser. This is the minimum verification workflow, not an admin redesign.

Keep one principal-bound credential history, independent of Practice memberships. The same person may work at several Practices, but each signature still needs the correct membership, Case assignment and named Consent. A credential is identified by regulator, jurisdiction, professional category and licence number; the number alone is not globally unique. Conflicting claims to the same credential across principals remain pending for identity review, never resolved by email matching.

1. **Request:** an admitted, verified-email, 18+-attested person supplies their professional name, category, regulator, licence number and jurisdiction. This creates a pending request, not verification or signing authority. A Practice administrator may nominate the person but cannot approve them.
2. **Bind the person:** a different cadet2 admin checks the authenticated principal against the named licensee. Confirm directly with the named professional through a business contact independently obtained from the regulator, or through an independently verified regulator confirmation. Applicant-supplied contact details, a shared reception mailbox alone, a licence image or knowledge of a public number are insufficient. Record the contact source, method, time, verifying actor and outcome; retain no password, challenge secret, call recording or new identity document by default. Missing identity evidence keeps the request pending. Repeat this step on material identity/contact/account changes or a credible impersonation concern.
3. **Look up the register:** the admin follows a configured official regulator directory, searches the exact licence number and reconciles name/category/jurisdiction. Open the individual entry and any linked restriction/order relevant to entitlement. Check practising entitlement, class, conditions, effective dates and any known expiry, suspension, revocation, surrender or other limit. A search hit or firm website alone is insufficient.
4. **Capture and decide:** preserve a minimal text extract or cropped capture of the relevant register fields/order, its official URL/entry locator and checksum. Record raw regulator labels alongside the normalized outcome and the policy version used to interpret them. A favorable decision requires complete matching identity and clear permitted scope; ambiguity, an unavailable register or an unrecognized label denies signing. No “approve anyway” or free-text override exists.
5. **Commit:** the guarded mutation rechecks the admin's live platform grant, non-self relationship, expected request/credential revision and evidence completeness. Append the immutable observation/decision, update the effective verification pointer/fences, and commit required audit and expiry/recheck intent atomically. Missing audit or a stale admin grant means no approval. Repeated identical operation keys return one outcome; changed inputs under the same key conflict.

For a positive check, use a server-started review window of **15 minutes**: the admin attests they performed the lookup during that window, records the observation instant within it, and commits before it closes. Server completion time remains separate. This bounds a manual assertion; it does not cryptographically timestamp the regulator's page. Old captures may support history but cannot renew verification. Adverse information can always be recorded to deny work without waiting for a fresh favorable lookup.

| Evidence responsibility | Required record |
| --- | --- |
| Identity and credential | Stable principal reference; professional name as displayed; category; regulator and province/territory or federal regulator jurisdiction; licence number exactly as published plus a regulator-specific comparison key; identity-binding receipt/reference. Never infer category from a number prefix. |
| Lookup | Official directory and individual-entry URLs/locator; observation and server-recorded instants; captured relevant fields/order and their digest; publication/effective dates where stated, explicit unavailable otherwise; admin principal and platform-grant revision. No Client/Case payload is needed in this dossier. |
| Entitlement | Raw status, class/type, entitled-to-practise indicator where provided, restrictions/conditions/order references and effective intervals, known licence expiry/cutoffs, permitted scope and the jurisdiction-policy version used. “Not published” differs from “unrestricted” or “never expires.” |
| Decision | Pending/verified/rejected/held/stale outcome and reason, prior observation link, policy version, `validUntil`, credential revision, idempotency key and audit reference. A new decision never overwrites a past observation or its date. |

Store verification evidence privately in the approved Canadian boundary, separate from Practice Case content. The applicant can see their own status and actionable reason, not verifier credentials or unrelated security details; the Client receives only the public signer projection below. Minimal historical verification evidence needed to explain a signature is retained by reference under its own approved audit schedule, not copied wholesale into every Case. Account deletion preserves only the disclosed necessary signer/verification receipt, not a permanent private dossier.

## Cadence, changes and lapsed verification

For this small pilot, a favorable manual register observation is usable for **at most 30 days**, ending sooner at any known credential/order cutoff or policy expiry:

`validUntil = min(observedAt + 30 days, known credential/condition cutoff, applicable policy cutoff)`.

Signing requires `serverNow < validUntil`, complete evidence, the effective verified revision and no hold. At equality it denies. The signing ceremony also requires the Representative's own attestation that their licence is in good standing and unrestricted for this work at the moment of signing (attestation text pending counsel), recorded in the Plan signature. The 30-day interval is a revisable operational limit, not a statutory period, continuous monitoring claim or source-citation freshness rule. It was set by the orchestrator on 2026-11-17, overruling an initial 24-hour limit: with manual lookups and a single pilot operator, a daily recheck would block ordinary signing, while the Representative's professional duty, the in-ceremony attestation and immediate holds on any known change bound the residual risk. Queue a recheck before expiry; if no admin is available, signing waits. Reverification repeats the register lookup and appends new evidence; it never extends the old date.

Require immediate review on a reported/licence/name/regulator/contact change, public adverse notice, contradictory register information, credible impersonation concern or policy change. The Representative has a versioned duty-to-report attestation, with final wording pending counsel. Staff/Practice administrators may submit a concern but cannot approve reinstatement. A credible concern can trigger an audited protective hold before its final disposition; do not publish an unverified allegation as an established regulatory fact.

| --- | --- | --- |

Otherwise active, consented, assigned members may prepare in their permitted role without implying current professional verification. An adverse professional status is not a legal ruling that every clerical/custody activity is prohibited; the professional-work hold is the pilot's conservative mechanism pending a documented scope decision. A harmless check-expiry does not cause that hold. Other authorized members can continue preparation and a newly assigned verified Representative can review/sign, only after the Client explicitly includes any new recipient under Consent. An admin cannot appoint themselves, restore withdrawn Consent or release work on the departed person's behalf.

The evaluator checks the verification/hold revision and server time on every signing admission/commit. Expiry mutations with expected revisions invalidate idle subscriptions/intents; old expiry tasks cannot stale a newer check. DTOs carry the earliest applicable `validUntil`; clear signing affordances at expiry/disconnect and revalidate on reconnect. Scheduler delay never permits an expired signature. Restore must replay credential/hold revocations from the independent Canadian recovery journal before enabling access/signing; a missing completeness watermark keeps the environment disabled.

## Exact content and signature binding

Freeze a **Client edition** from the authorized assessment and selected edits before requesting signature. It contains the exact disclosed advice, attributed professional conclusions, as-used facts/provenance, included findings, citations/receipts, missing facts, negative findings and material limitations. Staff proposals become adopted content only through the signing Representative's review; preserve the proposal's authorship. Unknown engine coverage stays unknown, source verification remains distinct from licence verification, and no score/rank/confidence field enters the edition.

The preview and subsequent Client reader consume the same immutable edition and versioned renderer. They do not regenerate advice from the latest profile, engine result or template. Font/line wrapping and export layout may differ; advice text, ordering, attribution, omissions and evidence limits cannot. The preview shows the proposed signer block; the actual server signing time is supplied by the final receipt. Current warnings and supersession status are separately labelled later overlays.

| Bound component | Exact requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Plan and authority | Signature/binding schema version; plan ID/revision; Case, Practice and historical Client binding; expected predecessor/current-plan revision; effective Consent receipt, text/scope digests and relevant membership/assignment/authority revisions. At commit require current `case.prepare` plus `case.review_and_deliver` for this named signer and selected facts. A historic Consent ID alone grants nothing. |
| Client edition | Persist the allowlisted edition, its content-schema/renderer versions and exact canonical bytes/digest. State included/omitted fields. Retain every limitation material to an included conclusion; signing cannot hide an unknown/blocker or change urgent/out-of-scope routing. |
| Assessment/input | Assessment ID, exact revision and digest; submitted input-snapshot ID, schema version and digest; original run/standard creation and artifact-processing provenance, assessment date/context and adapter version. Compare with the Case's expected current submitted revision. A changed submitted Case input requires a new candidate; an unshared global-profile change does not rewrite this snapshot. |
| Findings and edits | Exact included finding/claim IDs; every included edit ID, revision, digest, author/role/time and adopted text. A fact correction creates a new input and assessment revision; it cannot be a hidden edit to a receipt. |
| Rules, sources and calculations | Every relied-on rule ID/version/content digest and effective interval; citation ID/claim/passage/URL/snapshot digest, retrieval/publication/effective dates, check ID, verifier and verified-as-of date; calculation/method version, inputs/steps/result or explicit unavailable/not-applicable reason. Preserve distinct dates per dependency. Never substitute the signature or fetch date for a missing source check. |
| Retained evidence | Self-contained material as-used facts with provenance, included authored findings and exact relied-on rule/source passages/receipts, plus their manifest/digests, retention-policy version and the consent/verification accountability references. Follow ADR 0004's included/omitted field manifest; no full raw input, assessment, private verification dossier or vault mirror by default. |
| Signature receipt | Stable signer principal/historical identity, professional name, selected category/licence/regulator/jurisdiction, Practice and membership at signing, immutable verification observation/reference/digest/status/class/checked-at/valid-until, official verify link, server UTC signing instant, reauthentication receipt, exact attestation text/version/digest, intent/operation ID, bound manifest digest and audit event reference. Preserve identity after account deletion only to the justified retained minimum. |

The full input/assessment digests bind their original revisions **without requiring full raw snapshots to survive deletion**. Retain the as-used material evidence projection needed to understand the signed advice. Once an unretained source is deleted, its digest remains a commitment to the original, not a claim that the omitted fields can be recovered or recomputed. Reads/exports never fall back to live profile/vault joins to fill an unavailable historical source.

Use UTF-8 JSON Canonicalization Scheme ([RFC 8785](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8785)), SHA-256, explicit schema versions and fixed domain prefixes for three different digests:

1. `contentDigest`: canonical frozen Client edition, excluding the not-yet-created signature receipt and later warnings. This supplies the assessment contract's `SignedPlanContentBinding.contentDigest`.
2. `bindingDigest`: canonical manifest containing that content digest and all bound IDs/revisions/digests above, including the retained-evidence manifest. The signing intent names this digest.
3. `recordDigest`: canonical final receipt plus binding digest, excluding its own digest field. It binds the authoritative signing time, identity and verification snapshot to that reviewed content.

Prefix the canonical UTF-8 bytes respectively with `cadet2.plan-content.v1\n`, `cadet2.plan-binding.v1\n` and `cadet2.plan-signature-record.v1\n`, where `\n` is one LF byte; encode the SHA-256 result as lowercase hexadecimal. Keep displayed sections, tasks and evidence in the frozen edition's order; sort manifest reference sets by stable ID, then numeric revision where applicable. Required arrays are explicit, unavailable fields use their schema's explicit null/reason, and new server instants use UTC ISO 8601 with millisecond precision. Preserve source date precision/absence separately.

Reject duplicate object keys/IDs, unsupported/nonfinite or unsafe integer values, missing required fields and dangling references. Preserve reviewed strings without later Unicode, whitespace, markup or date normalization; schema conversion occurs before preview. Fixed independent canonical-byte/digest vectors must prove this encoding before delivery. Changing any bound content, edit, citation, rule/check, applicability, Consent or authority revision invalidates the intent. Rechecking a source creates a new dependency revision and review, not an update inside signed bytes.

Every view/export verifies the retained edition, manifest, receipt and material-artifact checksums before disclosure. A mismatch or missing required retained component makes the artifact unavailable and opens an integrity review; it must not be rendered as an authentic original with a warning. Metadata/status and rights-request paths remain available. Deleted, explicitly omitted raw sources do not prevent verification of the retained projection, and their original full-snapshot digest cannot be recomputed without those bytes.

At signing, recheck current rule/source usability and dependency revisions as well as credential validity. A claim relying on verified evidence cannot use a stale/withdrawn/unverified dependency. An explicitly limited unknown/referral plan may be signed after rebuilding/reviewing it with the actual limitations; it cannot retain a falsely verified claim. A rule-warning change during review requires fresh review even if the old captured passage is unchanged.

These are application evidence and integrity digests. They are not a personal cryptographic certificate, trusted timestamp, notarization, government filing, service agreement, Client signature or representation-appointment form. Application append-only controls do not establish WORM storage or non-repudiation against a malicious deployment operator. The legal effect and any stronger mechanism required are **pending counsel**.

## Single-plan signing ceremony and atomic commit

1. **Open review:** current authority admits the assigned Representative to one Case. Validate standard provenance and content/evidence requirements, freeze the complete candidate and retained package privately, and show the entire exact Client edition, material unknowns/limitations, edit authorship and selected licence. No Staff/admin “release” action substitutes for review.
2. **Prepare intent:** record a session/principal/Case-bound, single-use signing intent containing the binding digest, exact dependency/authority revisions, predecessor pointer and a server expiry. It lasts at most **5 minutes**. It confers no Client access and cannot outlive any authority/verification cutoff. Large immutable artifacts must already be durably available and checksum-verified; an incomplete package cannot be signed.
3. **Reauthenticate:** require an actual fresh credential challenge through the selected auth system for the same person/session and intent. A passive token renewal, recent page load, typed name, unlocked cookie or client-supplied `reauthAt` is insufficient. Store only the server-verified method/event reference, principal/session, time and intent binding; never a credential/token. The proof is at most **5 minutes** old at signing, single-use for this intent, and bound to the resulting admitted session if authentication rotates it. Missing supported reauthentication proof blocks signing; it does not fall back to a timestamp flag.
4. **Confirm:** show two unchecked, explicit attestations, adoption and current good standing, and a deliberate “Sign this plan” action for that candidate alone. Both must be accepted in the same request; a missing, unchecked or refused good-standing attestation denies the signature and invalidates the intent. Use same-origin/CSRF protection and server-held intent/proof binding, with no reusable proof in a URL or local storage. The account is never switched silently. A checkbox is evidence of an asserted review, not proof of comprehension or a substitute for professional responsibility.
5. **Commit:** one guarded Convex mutation derives the actor and server time; checks the live component session/principal/age gate, exact Practice/Representative membership and Case assignment, Client-self-signing prohibition, current granular Consent, Case state, chosen credential/hold/policy, expiry, intent/proof and all content/evidence/dependency revisions. Recompute/verify digests. Atomically consume intent/proof, create the immutable Signed plan and receipt/retained manifest, assign the consent decision's exact record-specific retained grants/custody, advance the expected current-plan/supersession pointer, make the signed Client edition available through Client-own rights, and append required audit/notification intent. Any failed check/write means none of these commits.
6. **Acknowledge:** return the committed plan/signature IDs, digests and time. Clear sensitive review/authentication state. The Client portal reads only that stored Signed plan projection. Rendition/notice workers may process the committed artifact under current authority; they cannot create signatures, release a Draft or change signed content.

Draft attestation, always displayed **pending counsel** until approved:

> I have reviewed this exact Client edition for this Case, including its stated facts, sources, dates and limitations. I adopt the included advice under the licence shown and intend to sign this revision.

> As of this moment, my licence shown above is in good standing, I am entitled to practise without any suspension, restriction or condition that would prevent me from giving this advice, and I have disclosed any change affecting my authority to practise.

Counsel must settle the final text, its legal meaning, disclosure of outside assistance, licence/status reporting duties and any separate engagement/appointment requirements. Store the rendered text of both attestations actually accepted, each with its own version and pending-counsel/synthetic status, inside the Plan signature receipt. No bulk signing, multi-Case checkbox, standing approval, delegated signature, automated re-signing or worker/import replay is permitted. Neither a Practice administrator nor a cadet2 admin signs by that capability; an admin who is also a Representative needs independent verification by another admin and all ordinary Case gates.

For retries, bind the operation key to principal, Case, intent and request digest. An identical retry after a lost response returns the original committed outcome, subject to current permission to read that outcome; it never creates a signature after revocation. A changed payload under that key conflicts. One plan revision has one signature: a duplicate intent or two Representatives racing for it yields one commit and an already-signed/conflict result for the loser. Two different proposed revisions also compare-and-swap the same predecessor/current-plan pointer; the loser must reopen review. There is no last-write-wins signature or unreviewed co-signing.

Sign/withdraw, sign/member-removal, sign/credential-hold and sign/edit races serialize through the authoritative mutation's dependencies. Denial/change first means no signature or retained package. Signature first preserves its exact retained record; subsequent denial suppresses dependent unsent dispatch and future work without erasing the Client's independently permitted signed retrieval. The UI must not display success on a timeout; recover the committed operation status. Audit failure cannot leave a published plan without evidence.

## Client-visible signer block and current warnings

Every Client Signed plan view and faithful export includes:

- Signer's recorded professional name, RCIC/lawyer designation, selected licence number, named regulator and jurisdiction, Practice at signing, plan revision and server signing date/time with timezone.
- “Licence checked on [observation date/time]” and the official **Verify this representative** link. Link to the stable individual entry where supported; otherwise the official search page plus a copyable licence number. Use configured HTTPS regulator hosts, no ephemeral admin-session URL, arbitrary applicant website, Case/Client identifier or Client referrer in the outbound request.
- The plan's assessment/as-used date and each relied-on rule/source version, effective dates and verified-as-of dates, with explicit unavailable values and evidence limits. Licence-check, source-check and signature dates have separate labels.
- A distinct present-status line: last known credential check/status and its observation time, stale/unavailable/restricted status where applicable, rule-usability warnings, and any correction/supersession/dispute notice. Do not disclose the admin's identity, identity-contact evidence, private licence dossier, security notes, Staff-only drafts or unadopted edits. Use factual attribution, without platform endorsement, approval-of-advice badges, guaranteed eligibility or success claims. A broken/unavailable register link keeps the recorded name/licence visible and offers the regulator's official directory/contact route; it never changes verification to successful.

Recheck current Client/retained rights before every view, export publication, download/Range/retry. ## Corrections, supersession and disputes

A Signed plan, its receipt and sealed evidence are immutable. Even a typo in signed prose or a wrong signer name/licence requires a new candidate and signature; a credential/profile edit cannot repair the past receipt. Use an appended dispute/correction notice immediately where needed, clearly separate from advice and the signed bytes.

Corrections cite the predecessor, reason and changed facts/content/evidence. Material input corrections require a new submitted snapshot and assessment revision. Text-only corrections may reuse unchanged immutable dependencies but still produce a new content revision, review and signature. Any replacement signer must have current verified authority, assignment and named Consent; a departed signer cannot be substituted by editing the name field.

A **Superseding plan** explicitly identifies the earlier Signed plan and replaces the Case's current-plan pointer atomically with its own signature. Preserve both; show the old one as superseded with the new signed revision link where the viewer has access. Until that transaction succeeds, no new unsigned candidate is Client-visible and no failed attempt can mark the original replaced. A withdrawal/dispute warning may say the old plan should not be relied on without exposing draft replacement advice.

After withdrawal, a Client can lodge a factual-dispute/rights request; authorized custodians may append its disposition under the independent retained policy. New personalized advice or a new signature requires renewed live Case authority/Consent, not a retained-record grant. If no current signer or custodian is available, keep the warning/restricted state and use the existing nomination/operator-continuity procedure. Legal obligations to notify, preserve, transfer, correct or restrict a disputed artifact remain pending counsel; never manufacture an amendment signature for an unavailable person.

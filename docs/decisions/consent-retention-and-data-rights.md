# Per-Case consent, retention and data rights

Decision date: 2026-11-17. Status: **answered-on-auto** for the private staging-pilot specification. Resolves [Decide per-Case consent, retention and data rights](https://github.com/wescodess/cadet2/issues/36) under [Chart the Client and Representative portals for a staging pilot](https://github.com/wescodess/cadet2/issues/29). This specifies behavior; it implements no rights workflow and grants no legal clearance or permission to use real Client data.

**Keep Client-owned information, live Case sharing and Practice-retained records separate.** Consent names one Case, purposes, recipients, actions and exact information. Withdrawal denies dependent access and work at its authoritative commit. It does not withdraw a Client's own rights, erase an already Signed plan or grant the Practice continuing access to a live profile. Account deletion removes all other personal data through a durable, reconciled cascade; retained exceptions and recovery-copy expiry are reported separately.

This branch starts from `origin/decide/tenancy-and-authorization` at `98dd1512f4de8c758a5dc70cbba3d657afbae935`, rather than directly from `dev`, to preserve its glossary and ADR 0003. Its code baseline remains `dev` at `fe2b4a4e4f8b4b861fb5c92661b9e411a84ef0b3`. [ADR 0004](../adr/0004-separate-retained-records-from-live-sharing.md) records the retention boundary: changing it after signatures exist requires a meaningful data/rights migration, retained personal information after account deletion would otherwise be surprising, and this deliberately rejects both whole-Case archival and deletion of the Practice's signed evidence. Other controls below are revisable specification choices, not additional ADRs.

## Three information boundaries

| Boundary | Owner and allowed use | Effect of Case withdrawal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Client account/profile and solo utilities | Client-owned identity/profile, checklist/vault, user-entered dates, reminder preferences and general information. No synthetic Practice or Case. Account/privacy acknowledgements and optional utility delivery choices are separate from Case Consent. | Own access continues. No Practice may browse it; independent solo reminders continue unless separately cancelled. |
| Live Case work | One immutable Client–Practice binding, selected profile facts/revisions, Case intake, explicitly shared document versions, Draft assessments/edits and Case reminders. Every workforce action needs the exact current grant plus the tenancy checks. | Dependent workforce reads, edits, processing, signing, exports and delivery stop. Data is inaccessible to workforce, then deleted or kept only for the Client's own permitted retrieval under the schedule. Withdrawal alone is not an immediate deletion instruction. |
| Practice-retained record | A sealed Signed plan and the minimal supporting/custody evidence specified below, with independent retained-record permissions and retention basis. | Retention/read/export continue only under this separate policy. No live lookup, unfinished work, new advice or new sharing follows from retention. |

Sharing is a deliberate copy of selected facts into a Case snapshot, not a pointer that follows the current profile. Every selected document has a version and Case sharing entry; sharing a folder or uploading a newer version grants nothing automatically. Two Cases, even at one Practice, have distinct snapshots, recipient lists, Consent and work. A Client can withdraw from Practice A while keeping Practice B and solo utilities active. No Case transfer or employment change transfers the Client's identity, Consent or file to another Practice.

Solo utility processing has its own disclosed, feature-specific purposes and express choices where sensitive data is involved: store the selected checklist/files or user-entered dates and provide the chosen reminder channel. A terms acknowledgement alone does not grant those purposes. Collect only the fields needed for the selected utility; using a deadline tracker does not require a complete immigration profile. Utility withdrawal/deletion stops its dependent processing; Case withdrawal does not. Moving a utility fact/file into a Case requires fresh explicit Case sharing, never a silent change of purpose.

## Consent scopes, purposes and receipt

Present separate, unchecked choices with the named Practice, named assigned recipients and roles, selected facts/document versions, purpose, actions, consequences of refusal/withdrawal, retained-record boundary, Canadian processor disclosure and privacy contact. A connection invitation, terms acknowledgement, valid session, upload or continued use is not Consent. A Practice cannot opt in for a Client, including by selecting a Client ID. No grant permits marketing, lead selling, third-party analytics, model training, an LLM prompt, external AI search or public indexing of Client information.

These identifiers express policy responsibilities, not final table/API names:

| Grant | Information and permitted actions | Dependency/refusal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `case.prepare` | Express processing/sharing of the selected Case facts for intake preparation and professional assessment: named assigned Staff/Representatives may read, prepare/submit Case intake, run the deterministic assessment and prepare attributed edits. List sensitive immigration/financial categories and individual selected fields explicitly. No global-profile edit, entire-vault read, signature or Client Draft disclosure. | Required for live workforce preparation; missing sensitive facts remain missing/unknown. Refusal leaves the connection unshared and solo utilities available; it is not consent to a smaller inferred scope. |
| `case.review_and_deliver` | The named assigned Representative may review the selected work, sign the exact approved revision and make that Signed plan available to the Client. Disclose the resulting Practice-retained record and its independent future custody/read/export purposes before assent. | Requires `case.prepare` and signing/verification gates. Refusal permits only otherwise authorized preparation, with no signature or personalized Client output. A retention disclosure is not a revocable promise to erase signed evidence. |
| `case.documents` | Read/download/use only individually selected, available, scanned document versions as evidence for this Case. Each entry states permitted view/download actions and purpose. An export of raw bytes requires that download action, not just metadata visibility. | Optional and granular per version; never required for every vault item. Missing evidence may prevent a conclusion; it does not widen a grant. Requires the applicable live preparation/review scope. |
| `case.reminders` | Share selected Case dates and permit named assignees to prepare/manage those Case reminders. External generic notices also require the Client's separate current channel preference and the approved transport. | Optional; declining does not block intake/signing. Withdrawal cancels Case-derived occurrences/outbox work, not independently created solo schedules. No account-wide reminder/calendar or email-preference access. |

Each scope binds an allowlist of fields/categories, immutable snapshot/document revisions, recipient principal/membership IDs, permitted actions and purposes. Whole-profile or “all current/future staff” selectors are prohibited. Initial Consent can establish the first named Case assignment atomically. Adding an assignee or signer, a field/category, a document version, a purpose or an action requires a new affirmative Client selection. A Representative's assignment-management action cannot widen the recipient list. Role/membership restrictions can narrow it immediately.

Store an immutable consent-text version (rendered English text plus digest), privacy/terms/retention-disclosure versions, the structured scope and its canonical digest, Client principal, Case/Practice, server timestamp, current revision, status, explicit expiry policy, attestations and idempotency key. Store the exact version the Client saw, not just a URL that can change. Do not collect IP addresses, device fingerprints or new identity documents merely to prove a checkbox. Acknowledging terms is recorded separately from granting individual purposes. Draft text might say “Share these selected facts with [named recipients] at [Practice] to prepare this Case”; every such draft must display **pending counsel**. No final legal text is adopted here.

The current pointer selects one effective scope revision; prior receipts remain immutable minimal audit evidence. States distinguish no grant/proposed, active, superseded, withdrawn and expired. A denied action never defaults a missing purpose to true. The approved policy supplies whether a calendar expiry is allowed/required and its duration; an absent policy is not an indefinite grant. For synthetic staging, use finite, explicitly synthetic fixture values and exercise expiry; production values remain pending counsel.

## Grant, change, withdrawal and re-consent

1. **Grant:** the server derives the Client from a current admitted session; checks age/authority attestations, exact Case binding, valid connection/invitation and expected revisions; validates the selected text/scopes; then commits the receipt, effective pointer, permitted initial assignment, audit and any work intent in one Convex mutation. Failure to commit required audit means no grant. An identical operation-key retry returns its committed receipt; changed selections with that key conflict.
2. **Change:** increasing scope requires affirmative assent to a new receipt/text version. Reducing scope commits a new denial/revision immediately. Every scope change fences work carrying an older revision, even where some permissions remain; new work is explicitly requested against the new revision. A new legal-text version never silently upgrades old Consent. Material changes require re-consent before the changed use; unchanged purposes may continue only if the published policy explicitly preserves that old version. Withdrawal remains available when the new text is declined or the policy service is unavailable.
3. **Withdraw:** the Client can withdraw a purpose, document share or the whole Case without a Practice's approval or deleting their account. One mutation changes the effective scope/denial revision, deactivates the dependent live assignments/work authority and commits the audit plus cleanup intent. A lost response is resolved by reading that operation's outcome; the UI must not report successful withdrawal before acknowledgement. Backend unavailability is an error/pending outcome, not a false success.
4. **Re-consent:** record a fresh receipt, current text and selected data/recipients after current attestations and Case/invitation checks. It can reuse the same Case only if the lifecycle decision permits; otherwise create a new explicitly connected Case. Explicitly activate the approved assignments. Never resurrect withdrawn scopes, old invitation credentials, signature intents, exports, queued processing or reminders by replaying an old receipt or merely toggling a status.

Purpose-only withdrawal fences the actions/data that depend on that purpose; any remaining explicitly granted scope is evaluated independently. Removing preparation also removes its dependent document/review authority, while an independently authorized reminder scope may operate only on its own selected dates. Whole-Case withdrawal removes all live scopes. It does not remove independent Client-own or retained-record entitlements.

The authorizer reads current receipt/pointer and revision as reactive dependencies alongside the tenancy/session records. Incomplete receipt, unapproved policy, stale revision, expired Consent, suspended membership or a mismatched Case/resource denies the dependent action before payload reads. Policy denial never blocks the Client's own withdrawal/status/rights-request path. Scheduled expiry mutations invalidate timed grants using expected revisions; every request checks server time and clients clear views at their `validUntil`, including while disconnected, exactly as the tenancy contract requires.

| Concurrent or pending activity | Effect at withdrawal's commit |
| --- | --- |
| Open portal, subscriptions, search/counts/history, cached SSR | Re-evaluate current authority to denied/redacted. Clear scoped rendered data/caches and reject late responses from the old authority epoch. Other independently granted Cases remain usable. A retained view must reopen through its separate admission, not keep the live subscription alive. |
| Queued/running assessment, edit, upload/scan or refresh | Fence attempts and cancel dependent work intents. Recheck before each protected read, external effect and final publication. A late result or scan receipt cannot publish, restore a share or become a Signed plan. Orphan/quarantined bytes are reconciled for deletion. Client-only vault processing may continue under its independent utility authority. |
| Signature screen, prepared approval or delivery | Invalidate unsigned review/signature intents. Interactive signing rechecks the exact Consent/input/edit/evidence revisions in the signature mutation. If withdrawal commits first, signing fails and creates no retained plan. If signing commits first, that immutable plan/evidence survives as retained; an unsent Case notification is cancelled. The Client may retrieve their Signed plan through own rights, independent of continuing Case sharing. No worker or Staff signature. |
| Export or download | Invalidate live-Case export intents and unpublished renditions; publication, every new request and Range/retry recheck authority. A new retained-record export requires its own permission and purpose. Client-own exports use own rights; withdrawal cannot cancel them merely because a Practice lost access. Deletion/authority restrictions still invalidate their affected content. |
| Reminder/outbox/provider operation | Suppress occurrences tied to the withdrawn scope before dispatch; old scheduled invocations/callbacks cannot reopen them. A send admitted before the commit may already have reached the provider. Preserve `send_unknown` and reconcile rather than blindly resend. Generic delivered notices and already admitted/downloaded bytes cannot be recalled. |

Withdrawal prevents new admissions/disclosures; it cannot erase a recipient's prior download, screenshot or in-flight response admitted before the commit. Private URLs stay behind the authenticated Canadian path; bucket ACLs, object keys, cookies and unexpired JWTs are not continuing permission. No durable private offline replica is added.

## Practice-retained record set

Seal the following manifest with the signature transaction. Retained payloads must resolve without reading a live profile, vault object or mutable Draft; large artifacts may be staged privately, but signing cannot succeed until all required content is durably available and bound to the manifest. Copies/renditions inherit the same retention class. Account deletion cannot follow a live foreign-key cascade into this boundary.

| Retained component | Included | Excluded |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Signed plan | Exact Client-disclosed signed content, signature, content digest/version, signer name/licence/regulator/verification reference/date, rule/source dates, citations/receipts, disclosed material limitations and subsequent correction/supersession pointers. Preserve recorded Client identity only as needed in that signed content. | Unsigned revisions, omitted judgments/Staff notes, entire raw engine result, hidden score/rank fields, current profile or current licence dossier. |
| Bound signing evidence | Material as-used facts and their provenance; included finding/edit revisions and authorship; exact relied-on public rule/source passages/calculation evidence; immutable binding and source digests. Store the subset actually supporting the Signed plan with an explicit manifest of included/omitted source fields. | Full input snapshot merely because it has a reference/digest; every historical assessment; unused facts; full private verification evidence. A source digest proves a binding, not that an omitted source is still available. It must never trigger a fallback live fetch. |
| Minimal Case/consent/custody audit | Who performed which transition, when, Case/Practice/record IDs, relevant text/scope versions and digest, selected recipients/actions, assent/withdrawal/authority evidence needed to explain disclosure, signature/publication/view events, correction/dispute, retained access/export and deletion/hold receipts. Actor identity snapshots are limited to accountability needs. | Full before/after profile payloads, free-text dumps, passwords/tokens, IP/device histories, raw message bodies or unrelated governance events. Event integrity does not justify payload hoarding. |

**Raw vault files are not retained by default**, including an upload used while preparing advice. The pilot retained package contains the signed content and material evidence projection above, not general document attachments or a permanent vault mirror. A signed document reference records the as-used version/checksum and verified facts; it is not a future download grant. A future proposal to retain original evidence documents requires an explicit file class, disclosure/basis, scope, schedule and counsel review before real-data activation. If professional rules require a broader file than this pilot models, activation is blocked until that conflict is resolved; do not secretly preserve the whole Case or claim the minimum package satisfies all professional rules.

A Case that never produced a Signed plan retains only the minimal consent/withdrawal/access/deletion audit justified by its schedule or a valid hold. Its unsigned assessments and notes remain deletable. During the Client's chosen retrieval window they may keep their own submitted facts/documents, but receive no unsigned assessment or Staff notes.

## Retained read/export policy (the tenancy matrix's Retained column)

Use separate `retained.read`, `retained.export` and `retained.custody` actions on an explicit immutable record-set ID. Every admission still needs the real session, active principal/Practice, active same-Practice membership, explicit current record grant, allowed purpose, record availability and current retention/hold/disclosure policy. Signing status or a historical Case assignment alone is insufficient. Staff and verification-pending Representatives can read/export if expressly granted; current licence verification remains necessary for new signing, not for ordinary record custody. Suspension for security or an explicit retained-grant revocation still denies access.

The tenancy rule for a person who is also the Case's Client still applies: serve the Client projection, never a union with workforce/retained privileges on their own Case. Employment or custody on other Cases remains independent.

At signing, create record-specific read/export grants only for the currently active named assignees included in the Client's disclosed recipient scope, and custody responsibility for the signer, with the manifest and audit. A pending unsigned Case has no retained plan grant. These grants are independent of live Case Consent: withdrawal deactivates live assignments without fabricating a new retained right. Removed/suspended members lose retained access immediately and do not regain it automatically on rejoining. No grant follows a departing signer to another Practice.

| Requester/purpose | Allowed retained projection after withdrawal | Denied |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Named Staff/Representative with explicit read grant | The specified fixed plan, its bound material evidence and Case-safe audit, solely to maintain the professional record, answer a Client rights/correction request, or handle a documented complaint/regulatory/legal obligation. Select and audit the purpose. | Live profile/vault/intake/Drafts, another Case's records, new assessment/advice, marketing, general “service improvement”, or use for the member's new Practice. |
| Same member with explicit export grant | Authenticated, version-bound export of that record set for the same permitted purpose, identifying Practice, exporter, manifest, versions, historical status, current warnings and retention duties. Each download rechecks current grants. | Whole-Case/archive exports, public/bearer links, unrelated audit, raw vault bytes, a new unsigned correction, or arbitrary recipient overrides/email attachments. |
| Record custodian | Administer custody/retention requests for specified records, subject to the same read/export grants. Legal hold authority is separately evidenced below. | Signing authority, live Case reactivation or a general privilege to all Practice files. |
| Client with active account | Exact own Signed plan, signer's public signature evidence, their own submitted facts and the permitted disclosure/Consent/rights audit, through own read/export rights after withdrawal. No Practice membership or renewed Consent is required. | Private Practice notes, another person's restricted data, internal custody/security dossiers or raw Drafts. |
| Practice administrator / cadet2 admin by that capability alone | Minimal identifiers/status needed to propose a custodian replacement or administer the verified rights/security task. | Retained content, export, self-assignment or a general Case audit. |

Replacement custody does not require renewed live Consent: preservation would otherwise depend on a former Client reopening sharing. Require an active Practice administrator's nomination of an active same-Practice member, plus approval by a current record custodian who cannot approve a grant to themselves. Commit exact record IDs/actions, reason, evidence, revision changes and audit atomically. An administrator may propose, but nomination alone confers no content access. The replacement receives only the retained scope and disclosed custody purpose, not a live Case assignment. If no eligible custodian remains, keep access denied and use a dedicated operator recovery operation: verify the Practice authority/continuity evidence and an independent administrator nomination before granting the bounded custody/read/export scope. The operator cannot approve their own access or gain contents from recovery. No generic admin bypass or resurrection of old assignments is allowed. These requirements also apply when a Practice closes or its members all depart; professional transfer instructions remain pending counsel.

Retained access is auditable, including reads and exports. Use an audited, session-bound read/export admission intent before returning payload; an ordinary reactive query cannot append its own audit. Its subsequent query/download still rechecks all current dependencies and cannot treat the intent as a bearer capability. Audit failure denies new access. Minimize audit fields, paginate Case-safe history, and never expose foreign counts/search facets. Retention expiry denies ordinary reads/exports even before physical cleanup; holds preserve only their exact records and use their separately approved access restriction. Missing policy denies retained reads/exports. Finite synthetic policy fixtures enable the pilot proof; no pending-counsel production value is silently interpreted as permission.

## Client export and correction

Provide an authenticated, self-service **UTF-8 JSON export plus readable PDFs of already Signed plans**, packaged with available original Client-owned document bytes in a private ZIP. JSON has a schema version, generation/as-of time, environment, resource/revision manifest, file checksums, source/binding digests, omitted/unavailable items with reasons and per-item retention/deletion status. PDF is a rendition of the exact signed content, with recorded signature/evidence dates and present historical/currentness warnings; it cannot regenerate advice from current inputs. The detailed Signed plan print/copy presentation remains with [Define Signed plan export into a Practice's existing workflow](https://github.com/wescodess/cadet2/issues/44).

Include own account contact/profile revisions still held; own entered/submitted Case facts with provenance; checklist, vault manifest and owned available files; solo and permitted Case reminder schedules/preferences; connection/Consent/attestation history; own Signed plans, their signed evidence/disclosed citations and Client-safe audit; and rights-request/retention exception receipts. A full-account export spans all the Client's Cases as separate scopes. A Case export includes only that Case and explicitly selected own resources. Practice withdrawal does not deny the Client's own export entitlement.

Exclude credentials/password hashes, sessions/recovery/verification tokens and keys; other Clients/Cases; private licence-check dossiers; workforce administration/security/hold internals; raw Draft assessments/unsigned edits/Staff notes, including indirectly through comparison/history/engine dumps. The first classes protect security and other people's rights; the unsigned classes preserve the settled signed-only disclosure contract. Export Client-supplied facts separately from professional work so refusal of an unsigned assessment is not refusal of the Client's inputs. A broader verified statutory access request is a separate scoped rights review with a documented response/refusal basis **pending counsel**, never a raw database-download endpoint or a promise that the pilot self-service export discharges every legal access right.

Record an exact source manifest at export admission. Build privately; recheck current identity/rights, deletions, dependant authority, grants and manifest revisions at publication and every download/Range/retry. A changed/deleted/restricted source invalidates the affected export; rebuild or return unavailable with reasons, never silently publish a stale ZIP or a partial success. Quarantined/missing/deleted files are named as unavailable; no scan bypass. Apply a finite configured artifact expiry; delete renditions on expiry, account deletion or loss of their authority. Avoid emailed archives/public URLs. Do not require an export to finish before accepting withdrawal or deletion; downloading a copy first is an option, not a barrier.

A Client corrects their global profile as a new revision. It does not rewrite submitted facts, signed evidence or another Practice's copy. Sharing corrections to a Case is explicit and requires current scope; Staff propose Case corrections with authorship rather than edit the global profile. Material corrections require new inputs/review/signature under the assessment/lifecycle contracts. A withdrawn Client can still lodge a scoped factual-dispute/rights request: append its resolution/correction notice without mutating signed bytes or creating new unsigned advice. Restricted third-party information or disputed authority can make a whole signed artifact temporarily unavailable pending verified rights review; do not silently redact and present it as the original signed artifact. Exact exceptions and response times remain pending counsel.

## Deletion, tombstones and recovery

Distinguish **withdraw sharing**, **delete selected information/Case working data**, and **delete account**. A resource deletion follows its dependency/copy manifest: deleting a vault version denies every live Case share of that version and cleans its copies/derivatives; it cannot remove the material fact or signed content already sealed as a disclosed retained exception. Deleting one Case's working data does not erase independently supplied data in another Case or the Client's global utilities. Account deletion covers every Case and utility belonging to that Client, except specified retained records/holds/minimal receipts. A Practice cannot veto it to keep unfinished work.

The Client authenticates freshly under the configured recent-auth policy, sees the scope and retained exceptions, and confirms the request. A raw Better Auth delete endpoint, direct component operation exposed to the Client or SQL `ON DELETE CASCADE` must not bypass this workflow. Losing account access uses the independently verified privacy-request path, not an email-only subject match. Record scope and idempotency key; do not demand a reason for ordinary deletion.

1. **Fence first, atomically:** record the deletion request, scope/manifest revision, Client/Case/document denial fences, withdrawal events, cancelled work/export/reminder intents, retained exceptions and required audit in Convex. Whole-account deletion denies the principal and its sessions to domain access immediately. A bounded cleanup cursor may enumerate large inventories afterward, but the root fence already prevents new copies/processing. Advance the independent Canadian recovery journal before claiming the fence durably recoverable. Failed journal replication leaves recovery readiness pending, never access restored.
2. **Clean each store idempotently:** a dedicated rights worker acts only on the committed manifest, not on the deleted principal's old interactive Consent. It may continue cleanup after auth deletion. Leases/attempt fences, per-step receipts, bounded retries and reconciliation prevent stale callbacks or partial cleanup from reopening access. The worker cannot export content, change identity or widen its deletion scope.
3. **Report truthfully:** expose request status/receipts without requiring the deleted account to log back in. A short-lived request-bound status credential may reveal only minimized counts/states, not profile, file names or retained payload; a verified privacy channel handles further requests. Do not keep an active auth account merely for a progress page. `pending`, `running`, `retrying` and `action_required` distinguish incomplete cleanup. Track live erasure, retained/held exceptions and backup expiry as separate results; “account disabled” or “auth removed” is not “all data erased.”

## 18+ and dependant authority

Require an explicit versioned **18-or-older attestation** before creating a usable portal account or collecting profile/intake/vault data; enforce it at the server, including direct calls. The same gate applies to workforce account activation; a professional role is not an inferred age attestation. Authentication plumbing may create a pending minimal principal, but it gains no Client/Case or workforce data access without the attestation and verified identity. Existing identities have no inferred attestation. Use a checkbox and server time; do not collect a new full birth date or identity document merely for this gate. If a legitimately supplied birth date contradicts it, stop further sensitive processing/sharing and route to a verified privacy/rights process. Refusal/under-18 status blocks portal use and offers public information/human help, not a child account or a false age default. Any inadvertently collected data enters the controlled deletion/hold process; a parent cannot turn a minor into the account holder.

Before collecting a dependant's identifiable information, record a separate versioned Dependant authority attestation identifying the dependant reference, Client, relationship, authority category and stated data/purpose/Case, time and scope revision. For a minor, attest parental/guardian or other legally valid authority; for an adult, attest that person's permission or another applicable legal authority, not merely marriage/household membership. Do not require uploading proof by default; sufficiency, disputed custody, adult incapacity and proof exceptions remain pending counsel. Missing/declined/disputed authority prevents new identifiable dependant data collection/sharing/export; collect only genuinely necessary non-identifying counts/age bands where the intake contract allows. Reaffirm authority before a new Case/purpose or material dependant-scope change.

Attestation does not permit consent for another adult's own Case, create a dependant login or enlarge the pilot into family/refugee or other excluded programs. Minimize dependant facts/documents to the in-scope purpose. Withdrawal or a credible dispute fences the affected live data and work in each affected Case; review which attestations are affected rather than assume all independently authorized Cases share one grant. Delete nonretained dependant information on the scoped request/schedule. Signed records follow the restricted retained/rights/hold policy, including whole-artifact restriction where necessary; no overwrite of the original signed content. Escalate medical/criminal/urgent concerns to a human under the assessment contract, with no LLM/free-text classifier or new narrative medical/criminal collection prompt.

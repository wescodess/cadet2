## How to read each surface contract

Every row specifies purpose/entry, audience, data/actions, state profiles with additional states, legal registry references, next actor, device/accessibility requirements and an acceptance scenario. The row and **all** its inherited profiles are one requirement; a profile reference is not permission to omit a state. A named state is applicable whenever the selected resource reaches it, including through a bookmark, history navigation, notification or reconnect. A surface may present a bounded notice instead of a full workflow when that audience cannot act.

### Audience rules from the tenancy matrix

| Code | Who may see or act |
| --- | --- |
| PUB | Anonymous public-information projection only; no Client facts, invite details before exchange, private counts or assessment |
| SELF | Admitted person managing only their own account/security; verified email and explicit 18+ activation before protected domain use; includes workforce accounts |
| C | Client-own allowlisted facts, utilities, Cases and exact Signed plans. Solo Client has no fabricated Practice, Case, Draft or converter. An invited Client remains an independent account |
| W | Assigned Staff or Representative with active same-Practice membership, explicit resource/action assignment, exact current granular Consent and lifecycle authority. Pending/stale verification may allow preparation; a professional-work hold may deny it |
| V | Assigned Representative acting as reviewer; signing additionally requires current manual verification, selected usable credential, no hold, exact dependencies, fresh reauthentication and both attestations. Staff never perform professional review/signing |
| PA | Practice administrator capability: this Practice's membership/settings/invitations and minimal nominations/audit only. No Case contents, live self-assignment, retained read or signature from this capability |
| RET | Active same-Practice member with record-specific retained read/export/custody action and permitted recorded purpose, schedule/hold policy and audited admission. Former assignment/signature alone is insufficient |
| ADM | Current narrow cadet2-admin grant for verification, public-basis governance and minimized diagnostics. No arbitrary Case/profile/vault/Draft access, Case audit or signing |
| MSG | Only the intended currently authorized recipient; message templates and transport receipts are not a new resource grant |

Rights are evaluated per resource and audience, never unioned into a maximum role. A person who is also the Case's Client gets C on that Case and cannot review/sign it. PA or ADM users with an independent W/V/RET grant use that separate admission; neither admin role supplies it. Client, Practice, Case and child relationships are server-resolved. Every list, count, filter, search, comparison, SSR payload, export and download inherits the same rule.

### Feature dependencies

Private Client entries require synthetic_pilot_access + client_portal; workforce entries require synthetic_pilot_access + representative_portal and their independent grants. Solo feature rows also require solo_utilities; scheduling/publication/dispatch also require deadline_reminders; bytes independently require vault_uploads or vault_downloads. Uploads off does not itself deny a permitted existing clean download. live_email remains off and selects explicit synthetic capture, not an error fallback. admin_ai_search remains off without its separate public-corpus/provider gates. self_serve_planner stays hard-off on every route/API/export; there is no solo conversion screen. No browser flag-management screen is added: operator-only flags.manage remains a bounded operational capability.

Public scope, legal documents, regulator links and ordinary rights routes remain available when productive features pause. General-information summaries under P05 additionally obey solo_utilities; static scope/help and official links remain available if summaries are off. ADM governance is not enabled by either portal flag and still obeys its own current authority/provider gates.

### Four status kinds and all lifecycle states

Every Case/list/task/notice/edition item identifies its selected Case, cycle/edition, as-of time, **assessment coverage; work; evidence; review validity**, and next actor/action. Do not collapse them into one “status”. Pure auth, account, membership, admin governance and public pages have assessment coverage and professional review **N/A**, not unknown/blocked; their own operation/evidence states remain explicit.

Coverage notation: C0 = no personalized assessment disclosed because no accessible Signed plan; CS = exact selected Signed plan findings; P0 = not yet assessed; PD = exact admitted Draft findings; PS = historical signed findings. C0/P0 are disclosure/work placeholders, not new claim statuses. CS/PD/PS contain per-subject supported, blocked, unknown or out_of_scope, with reasons, missing facts, receipt, citations and verified-as-of. Only evidenced language criteria can gain supported/blocked findings under IN; named-program engine coverage stays empty. Evidence EC = Client-own supplied/transfer/scan/review receipt without Draft conclusions; EP = authorized workforce checks/acceptance for an exact assessment/source; ES = signed as-used evidence, with separate current warnings.

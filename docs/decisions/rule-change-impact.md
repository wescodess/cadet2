# Minimum honest rule-change impact workflow

Decision date: 2026-11-17. Status: **answered-on-auto** for the private staging-pilot specification. Resolves [Define the minimum honest rule-change impact workflow](https://github.com/wescodess/cadet2/issues/43) under [Chart the Client and Representative portals for a staging pilot](https://github.com/wescodess/cadet2/issues/29). This record specifies behavior and first test seams; it does not implement impact detection or establish pilot readiness.

**A rule change is an admin-published change to a recorded assessment basis: a pathway rule, evidence source, CLB/NCLC conversion table or source verification state.** Match it only to dependencies actually recorded for a Case edition. An exact dependency change requires review; its effect on advice may still be unknown. Missing legacy dependencies mean **impact unknown**, never unaffected. Representatives review each Case; only a newly signed Superseding plan changes the personalized output delivered to the Client.

## Manual change input and publication

The minimum input is a human-authored change record published by a currently authorized cadet2 admin in the existing admin. A source approval permits its governed use; it is not confirmation that every passage is correct or that every Case is affected. An admin publishes basis changes and verification observations, not personalized advice or a professional resolution of a Case.

| Change kind | What the publication establishes |
| --- | --- |
| Pathway rule | An identified predicate, applicability, activation/retirement or correction changed from the retained old rule version to a new immutable version/tombstone. Generic demo-rule edits remain generic; they create no named-program capability or official requirement. |
| Evidence source | An identified relied-on passage, table, authority, snapshot or applicability changed or was corrected/withdrawn. Distinguish an evidenced requirement change from an editorial correction or a source replacement whose substantive effect is not yet established. |
| CLB/NCLC conversion table | An identified chart/rule version, skill row/range, score scale/column, cohort or result-use basis changed. Preserve the exact old/new cells, method and citations. Do not infer CRS or program eligibility from the change or combine test attempts. |
| Source verification state | An identified claim-source check became stale, withdrawn, unverified/unavailable or newly checked, with its reason and observation. This can require review even when source bytes are identical. It is not a statement that immigration law changed, and is unrelated to a licence check's 30-day limit. |

Every published record includes:

- Stable change ID/revision, kind, factual title/summary, reason, publisher and server UTC publication time; a separate reported observation time with provenance. Corrections reference their predecessor.
- Exact affected rule/source/check/chart identities, old/new immutable versions and digests, retained supporting content and locators, and the scope actually changed. Explicitly say when an old version or a new passage is unavailable; an unavailable side cannot support a precise before/after assertion.
- Old/new requirement or source/check state, verification evidence and verified-as-of where actually checked. An official-rule claim needs the assessment contract's usable evidence. For an unresolved observation publish a checking/verification-state notice, not an asserted new requirement.
- Evidenced effective date/interval, date precision, stated applicability/cohort/use and unresolved questions. Unknown dates stay unknown; observed-at, published-at, retrieved-at and effective-at are different. Future commencement is not commencement today.
- Admin follow-up owner and next action for missing source evidence, the publication's current dependency revision/fence, mandatory audit and durable matching intent. No Client facts, Case names, immigration answers or caseload list enters the shared change record.

Authoring has `draft` and `published` states, with a cancelled draft allowed. Draft edits do not silently change a usable rule/source. Publishing commits the new basis/version or usability restriction, change record, audit, dependency fence and work intent together in Convex. Lost-response retries recover the same operation receipt; changed content under that key conflicts. A safety withdrawal is effective at that commit, without waiting for a complete replacement rule. Old versions remain historical under their retention policy rather than being hard-deleted to simulate a release.

Published records are immutable. A mistaken announcement is corrected/superseded by another dated publication with its own evidence and matching pass. It cannot delete warnings, restore stale reviews or retract notices as though nobody saw them. Merely finishing the fanout does not close every Case review.

**No automated scraping, scheduled source monitoring, LLM summary/change detection or automatic legal-effect extraction is added.** An admin manually consulting an official source and retaining the relevant material is sufficient input. A hash/byte difference alone is an observation to check, not a verified policy change. A failed fetch/404/parser or retrieval failure cannot establish repeal, unchanged law or continued usability; record the actual uncertainty. Existing RAG ingestion/sync is not an impact detector and is not enabled or expanded by this workflow. No detection-latency or response SLA is promised.

## Recorded dependencies and honest matching

Capture dependencies with each new immutable assessment/edit and each frozen Client edition, under their existing contracts. A bounded reverse index is a rebuildable projection of those records, not a new rules graph or authority. It stores opaque scope/edition references and dependency identities; it does not index Client prose, full profile answers or vault bytes.

The dependency manifest resolves its stored Case–Practice–Client binding, edition/revision/digest and scope: current unsigned cycle, current Signed plan or a named historical Signed plan. Within it retain rule ID/version/content/applicability; claim/citation IDs with source/snapshot/passage/check identity and dates; conversion receipt/chart/calculator/attempt/skill/scale/cohort references; and the exact signed section/claim links where included. Dependencies of Representative-authored claims and their premises count as well as system findings. An omitted Draft claim does not become a dependency of the Signed plan. Record whether the manifest is complete for that edition/schema or partial/absent; an empty unrecorded list is not a deliberately complete manifest.

Matching is deterministic over these records and the published scope. It may inspect currently permitted, explicitly Case-selected applicability facts; it never polls the Client's global profile, infers a program from nationality or a generic pathway, or matches a source by hostname/name similarity. Alias resolution needs a recorded, evidenced identity mapping. Current mutable rows, legacy fallback citations, source tags, an evaluator label and a run date cannot reconstruct what an old run used.

| Result for an edition | Required basis and review meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Known dependency change** | Exact recorded old rule version, changed cited passage/check, or conversion receipt/table scope intersects the published change, with applicability established. Persist the exact references and why they match; lifecycle `change_known`. The **recorded basis** is affected; neither eligibility nor a changed numeric result is established. Unrelated missing dependencies remain disclosed. |
| **Potential match** | A recorded citation/source or receipt is relevant, but version, passage boundary, cohort/date or applicability is unresolved. For example, a recorded canonical source URL with no snapshot version is a lead, not proof. Persist that limited link and uncertainty; lifecycle `impact_undetermined`. A bare shared hostname or pathway category is insufficient even for this classification. |
| **Impact unknown** | The Case edition has missing/partial/unresolvable dependency history and no stronger established match. Legacy runs without recorded dependencies belong here. Persist the missing basis; lifecycle `impact_undetermined`. Require provenance/evidence review or a fresh authorized revision, never an unaffected result. |
| **No recorded match within the checked scope** | A complete admitted manifest has no intersection with this publication's identified scope, as of the recorded matching revision. This is only a scoped search result, not proof that a person, Case or immigration strategy is unaffected by a policy change. Never label it “unaffected” or imply program-wide monitoring. |

Keep all known matching reasons and uncertainty, not only the first match. An exact unusable check applies to every recorded reliance on that check even if its bytes did not change. A content change elsewhere on the same page is only a potential match until its relation to the retained passage is established. A chart update confined to another skill/column/cohort must not become an exact match to an unrelated conversion. An unchanged conversion number still needs review when its relied-on basis changed.

The matching universe is explicitly bounded by environment, authorized Case/retained-record scope, edition classes, manifest/schema versions and a captured revision/watermark. Report pending/running, completed-at/through, failed or incomplete coverage separately from result counts. New admitted editions must reconcile against the latest published basis before review/signing and enter subsequent matching; no one-time scan claims coverage of future work. Index rebuilds use recorded manifests, and incomplete index coverage cannot produce a negative result. Unknown legacy editions remain visible as a review group even when the exact-match group is empty. Denied or unavailable reads are not successful zero-result scans.

Run IDs/user IDs alone do not identify a Case. Never invent a Case, Signed plan, signature or assignment for an orphan legacy run. Where an authorized stored link exists, show its missing dependencies as impact unknown; otherwise keep the unresolved legacy provenance outside the caseload, with a bounded operational reconciliation task. The assessment contract already rejects baseline runs lacking standard provenance; this workflow cannot make them admissible. A new check or reviewer-recorded link is dated now and belongs to a new review/revision, never a backfilled historical dependency.

## Minimum existing-admin and service responsibilities

This is **security fixes plus minimum workflows** for the existing admin, alongside its already-settled Representative-verification queue. Extend the existing pathway/rule, evidence-source and audit touchpoints with manual old/new capture, draft/preview/publish, reason/check evidence, correction, and matching-job status/retry. The conversion table's manual release must use the same evidenced publication path. No new admin redesign, monitoring dashboard, rule-graph editor, ingestion programme or general Case browser is authorized.

Apply the tenancy contract's platform grant and live session checks on direct Convex and Nitro routes, remove allowlist bootstrap/acting-as deployment-key bypasses, validate inputs, and migrate the authoritative writes/audit to Convex through the already-owned storage/security delivery. UI confirmation alone is insufficient. Administrative run/citation `resolved`, source approve/resume and AI Search sync cannot resolve impact review, reverify claims, sign or release a plan.

A narrow trusted matching job uses the committed change/attempt, cursor and dependency references. It may enumerate only the minimum governed dependency/edition metadata and write scoped review-need/warning intents after current live or retained-policy admission. It gains no live Case-content, profile, raw Draft or signing access from the publishing admin. Any applicability/content read requires the independent current Case grant or retained purpose. cadet2 admins receive only public-basis details and minimal permitted job diagnostics, not Case rows, cross-Practice counts or Client identities. Practice administration alone likewise sees no caseload.

Publication's root basis/usability revision takes effect before asynchronous fanout. Assessment capture, review readiness and signing check the actual current dependency heads, publication fence and completeness under the common evidence evaluator; they cannot rely on an unprocessed queue or an old ready flag. A relevant concurrent change invalidates a bound candidate even before its review row is materialized. Missing reconciliation denies verified reliance/signing until the candidate is rebuilt/reviewed; it never supplies unaffected status. The signed reader derives the matching warning or a checking/undetermined overlay from current dependency state while fanout is pending; it cannot show unchanged-current assurance because no row exists yet. Publication's durable work intent owns notice reconciliation. The existing authenticated view-admission mutation may materialize the same idempotent review need/notice before retrieval; a reactive reader never writes an audit, task or notice.

Each per-edition materialization commits `review_need.recorded`, warning/review revision, unsigned intent invalidation, minimal audit and permitted notice/work intents together. Use one idempotent identity per change revision, Case, edition and review need; retries do not duplicate notices/tasks. Commit progress only with the admitted writes. A crash resumes from its durable cursor/fence; unavailable authority leaves a bounded suppressed/pending outcome, not a success or a grant. Match completion, Case review disposition and external delivery are separate facts. Corrections/new dependency revisions reopen the relevant work; they cannot reuse an earlier reviewer disposition.

## Representative caseload and per-Case review

The Representative portal shows a dated change notice with kind, publisher/check provenance, old/new requirement or source state, evidenced effective dates or unknown, scope/limits, and matching coverage as of its recorded revision. Expand to **only the Cases the current person may access**, grouped first by review need and then by urgency. Totals/facets/history obey the same admission as rows; no global Practice-count shortcut or fallback to an admin review list.

| Review-need group | Owner and next action |
| --- | --- |
| Known dependency change | Existing permitted assigned Representative owns professional review; Staff may prepare evidence/edits. Open the exact affected section, retained old basis, new basis and matching explanation; establish the effect on this Case. |
| Potential match / impact unknown | Named permitted reviewer checks applicability or missing provenance. Request the specific missing fact/evidence or open a fresh authorized assessment/revision. Keep unknown visible while waiting. Do not dismiss the group because exact matches are zero. |
| Revision/review/signature underway | Link the one active unsigned cycle, its preparer/reviewer, current blocker and next actor. Show outstanding changes separately from completed checks; a second change cannot create a competing current cycle. |
| Individually reviewed / no recorded match | Show the exact dated disposition or bounded negative search scope, underlying evidence and remaining limits. A human disposition is distinct from a matcher result. Historical-only matches name the old edition and any accessible successor; they do not automatically taint an unrelated current plan. |

Urgency is a separate recorded dimension, never an inferred immigration risk score. Show the existing structured human gate (`required`, `undetermined`, `clear` at its assessment time), actual applicable status/deadline facts and their provenance, and the change's known commencement. Required human escalation gets the urgent group and real contact path; unresolved safety/date/applicability gets **urgency needs checking**, never routine by default. Within otherwise known groups, order by actual relevant recorded dates and then stable identifiers. A known future effective date can schedule review, but creates no Client legal deadline. Any human-set internal review target is labelled operational, with author/date, not law or a response promise. Re-evaluate the Toronto 90-day/expired-status gate as settled; a source change or review acknowledgment never clears it.

Each row identifies Case/edition, review need and reasons, affected signed sections where known, uncertainty, owner or explicitly unassigned, next action/actor, dated urgency basis, review state and notice state. No authorized assignee/contact means assignment/contact needed; do not invent a reviewer or on-call availability.

Closed/withdrawn Cases appear only through a separately admitted retained-warning/custody projection, with the actual record custodian and permitted next action. They cannot enter live assignment, reassessment or professional-review batches through that access.

Bulk assignment is allowed **only to existing active same-Practice recipients/actions already selected in each Case's current Consent**, through an authorized Case manager. Staff may prepare or request assignment; administrator nomination alone activates nothing. A new recipient requires affirmative Client selection before access. Each selected Case gets its own authorized transaction, expected revision, audit and success/conflict/denial receipt; a partially successful batch cannot be reported wholly assigned. Review, professional disposition and signature remain per Case and exact edition. No bulk “reviewed”, bulk dismissal, bulk re-signing or standing approval.

The assigned Representative records `in_review` only when they actually begin, then an exact-revision disposition: clarification/evidence needed, not applicable with evidenced reasoning, no plan revision required with evidenced reasoning, or revision required. Staff/admin/service cannot record that professional conclusion. Missing dependencies or unresolved applicability cannot be closed as no impact merely to empty a queue. An acknowledgment is not completion. A new change, submitted fact, included edit or check revision invalidates stale readiness/dispositions as the lifecycle requires.

A no-revision disposition documents what was checked and why no change to the signed edition is required; it never changes its original source dates, signature or engine coverage. The Client may receive an operational review-completed receipt, not unsigned new personalized reasoning or “safe/unchanged” assurance. Adopting a new source basis or changing any Client-facing advice, limitation, action, requirement or date requires a newly reviewed and signed edition, even if much of the wording is identical. If verified reliance is still unusable, its warning cannot be cleared by an admin resolution or no-revision checkbox.

## Client notice and historical plan

When a permitted review-need/warning is recorded for an accessible Signed plan, create its dated Client portal notice immediately with that transition, before a replacement plan exists. This is a currentness/work notice, not a new finding or advice. No Signed plan means no personalized rule-change result; the Client sees only their permitted work/receipt status. A historical-only match names that historical edition without claiming the current plan changed.

The notice names the Signed plan's version/date and **actual affected section title(s)** from its frozen edition, explains that those sections await Representative review, distinguishes an established basis change from potential/unknown impact, and names the actual permitted reviewer/next actor and known contact. It gives notice time separately from an evidenced effective date. If no section mapping can be established, name the Signed plan as a whole and say the affected section/impact is not yet known; never invent section precision for legacy records. Show reviewing only after the Representative's start receipt, otherwise awaiting review/assignment.

Illustrative operational content, **draft — pending counsel**, to join the legal-floor registry: “Notice recorded {noticed_at}. Section {actual_section} of your Signed plan dated {signed_at} is awaiting your representative's review because its recorded source basis changed. The effect on your plan is still being checked. The original signed version remains available.” The unknown variant says that the source basis/affected section cannot yet be established. Do not insert a new requirement, deadline, claim that the Client must immigrate/file/retest, assurance that waiting is safe, or a promised review time. Any actual Client action/advice comes from the subsequent Signed plan or the existing factual clarification/contact flow.

Keep the original signed bytes, receipt, signer/source dates and evidence visible under Client-own access, labelled as the historical basis awaiting review. The current-plan pointer remains on that exact Signed plan until a Superseding plan is signed; “historical basis” does not falsely mark it already superseded. Warnings and their later dispositions are dated overlays. Integrity/rights restrictions may make an artifact unavailable under the existing policy, never silently rewrite it or substitute a reconstructed original. Reader/history/deep-link/comparison/export paths must preserve the selected edition and warnings; no Draft comparison leaks the proposed revision.

Optional external notices follow the lifecycle outbox and current channel/purpose checks, with generic update-available text and a fixed authenticated portal-entry link. No rule name, section, Case/Practice label, deadline, immigration facts, plan attachment or personal URL parameters enter email. Initial delivery is Canadian synthetic capture, explicitly **not sent**, with no later flush. A provider acceptance is not Client viewing, acknowledgment or professional review; failure/`send_unknown` leaves the portal warning in place and uses reconciliation rather than blind resend. A new notice accompanies the later signed replacement through the same gates.

After withdrawal/closure, stop live preparation, review, signature and dependent Case sends. The Client's own permitted historical-plan/warning retrieval remains separate. Any retained warning/custody task uses the exact retained record, public dependency references and an independently authorized retained purpose; it cannot load live profile/vault/Drafts, widen grants or produce new advice. If policy does not authorize the operation, deny it and record the limited outcome. New personalized revision requires renewed live authority and Consent. After account deletion, use the governed rights path, not a re-created account with the same email. Legal notice duties and wording remain pending counsel.

## Revision, reassessment and supersession

1. **Record need:** publication and matching establish the review need/uncertainty and current warning; invalidate affected unsigned review/signature intents. The earlier Signed plan stays available under its own rights. No change publication automatically releases advice.
2. **Review one Case:** the current assigned Representative checks the recorded old basis, published new basis, applicability, selected facts, material limitations and independent escalation gate. A new check creates a dated evidence revision. Missing source material means unknown/unavailable, not a favorable default.
3. **Open or update the one unsigned cycle:** an authorized preparer uses `revision.opened` linked to the predecessor and exact change IDs. Coalesce further changes through explicit new revisions; fence/supersede stale unsigned work rather than create a parallel current cycle. New Case facts require a new deliberately shared submitted snapshot and assessment. Text-only corrections may reuse unchanged immutable dependencies; changed rules/charts/checks require fresh relevant assessment/receipt or attributed checked professional content, not mutation of the old assessment.
4. **Compare actual editions/bases:** show authorized fact changes separately from rule/source/check and conversion changes. Display original/new values, evidence, known dates and unavailable sides; no improved/declined prospect, scores or guessed historical data. A conversion rerun retains its chart/method/skill trace and limits; the legacy adapter's named-program coverage stays `[]`.
5. **Review and sign anew:** Representative review binds the new exact Client edition and current dependencies. Full membership/assignment/Consent, current usable licence check, holds, fresh reauthentication, both attestations, source usability and expected predecessor gates apply. A limitation/referral plan can be rebuilt and signed honestly; stale evidence cannot remain labelled verified. No admin source approval, run refresh or old signature bypasses these checks.
6. **Publish the Superseding plan atomically:** the signature transaction seals the edition/receipt/evidence, moves the current-plan pointer, records predecessor supersession, audit and notice intent together. Only then does the Client see the replacement and a link to its historical predecessor. Failed signing, an intervening change or a pointer conflict leaves the original untouched and the review need unresolved. Resolution records the signed edition and exact changes addressed; newer outstanding changes remain open.

## First test seams and synthetic proof set

Start test-first at a pure **published change + captured dependency manifests → explained match results** boundary, with explicit clock/scope and fixed evidence. It must return the matching reason/references, missing basis and scoped coverage, not just a boolean. The first negative control is a legacy manifest with no dependencies: removing the unknown guard must turn a required assertion red. This matcher is distinct from the shared evidence-usability/authority evaluators; it supplies no permission or eligibility verdict.

Use finite fictional rule/source/chart names and stipulated passages, never real applicants or invented immigration law. The small proof set includes:

## Glossary additions

**Rule change**:
An admin-published change to the pathway rules, source evidence, language-conversion tables or source verification on which an assessment or plan may rely. It does not by itself establish a change in law or in a Client's circumstances.
_Avoid_: Automatically detected policy change, eligibility change

**Recorded dependency**:
A rule version, claim citation or calculation/conversion basis preserved with an identified assessment or plan edition. A related topic or source is not proof of reliance.
_Avoid_: Inferred dependency, current source match

**Impact review**:
A Representative's examination of a published basis change or unresolved dependency for one Case edition. Assignment or acknowledgment is not its professional conclusion or a new signature.
_Avoid_: Admin resolution, bulk approval, automatic reassessment

**Impact unknown**:
The state in which the available dependency history cannot establish how a published change relates to a Case edition. Missing history supplies no assurance that the Case is unaffected.
_Avoid_: Unaffected, low risk, no change needed

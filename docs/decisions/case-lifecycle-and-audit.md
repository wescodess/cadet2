# Case lifecycle and audit transitions

Decision date: 2026-11-17. Status: **answered-on-auto** for the private staging-pilot specification. Resolves [Define the Case lifecycle and audit transitions](https://github.com/wescodess/cadet2/issues/38) under [Chart the Client and Representative portals for a staging pilot](https://github.com/wescodess/cadet2/issues/29). This record specifies behavior and first test seams; it does not implement the lifecycle or establish pilot readiness.

**A Case has separately governed work, Consent, signed publication and retention.** One current unsigned work cycle can progress while an earlier Signed plan remains available. Only the signature transaction publishes personalized Client content. Every authoritative transition commits its required audit and durable notification/work intents in the same Convex mutation. Neither processing completion, an admin review, an email nor a Client view grants authority or changes signed content.

## Model: independent state and exact revisions

These are semantic responsibilities, not a final schema or names for public routes. A single enum such as `completed` cannot represent a signed historical plan, a failed new draft and withdrawn sharing simultaneously.

| Record or axis | States and invariant |
| --- | --- |
| Connection proposal, before a Case exists | `pending`, `accepted_unshared`, `declined`, `cancelled`, `expired`, `connected`. Invitation delivery has its own receipt. An intended email is not a Client identity or a Case. Only explicit consented acceptance creates the Case with immutable Client/Practice bindings and first approved assignment. |
| Case disposition | `open` or `closed`, with expected revision. Open permits only otherwise authorized actions; closed denies new live work. Withdrawal, expiry and security restrictions are current authority conditions from their authoritative records, not a second Consent ledger. |
| Work cycle | `intake` → `submitted` → `draft` → `review` → `signing` → `signed`; `superseded` and `cancelled` are terminal unsigned outcomes. At most one current unsigned cycle per Case. A signed cycle stays signed; a revision opens a new cycle linked to it. Clarification and review dispositions can move work back as specified below. |
| Assessment processing | `queued`, `evaluating`, `completed`, `degraded`, `failed`, plus a separate work-intent cancellation fence. Completed/degraded becomes a Draft only if the adapter/contract and provenance admit an immutable captured revision. Failure/unavailability never becomes an unknown eligibility finding or Client advice. |
| Review disposition | `requested`, `in_review`, `changes_requested`, `ready_for_signature`, bound to exact input/assessment/edit/evidence revisions and reviewer. Staff can request review; only an assigned Representative records their own professional review. Readiness is neither a signature nor a release. |
| Human handoff | `not_sent`, `sending`, `received_by_service`, `awaiting_representative`, `acknowledged_by_representative`, `failed`, `outcome_unknown`; `route_unavailable` where no authorized recipient/contact exists. Record structured dispositions from intake without clearing `required`/`undetermined` escalation by acknowledgment. |
| Signed publication | An immutable Signed plan, signature receipt and sealed retained manifest; one current-plan pointer plus immutable predecessor links. Signature creates `available` portal publication. Restrictions, integrity unavailability, expiry and erasure are separately audited availability overlays, never edits of signed bytes. |
| Notice delivery and Client view | Outbox/capture/provider outcomes and authenticated view admission/acknowledgment are separate from publication. A mail-server receipt is not Client viewing; view acknowledgment is not reading, agreement or task completion. |
| Review validity | Per selected edition: no professional review, signed version, changed since review, superseded version; dispute/unavailable warnings qualify that state. An unresolved dependency yields impact undetermined, never a claim of unchanged validity. No global “valid/eligible” badge follows. |
| Retention and rights | Per-class schedule/manifest, due/held/restricted/erasure states and per-store receipts. `archived` is only a closed/retained navigation projection, never a grant, new copy, indefinite retention class or evidence of deletion. |

The Case names its current submitted snapshot, active unsigned cycle, current signed plan and revision. The active unsigned pointer is cleared or replaced atomically when its cycle becomes terminal. Every child resolves the same stored Case/Practice/Client. A cycle binds the submission/schema/digest, standard run and artifact provenance, assessment contract/version/digest (including `assessment.v1.language.2026-11-17` where used), attributed edit revisions, evidence/rule/check dependencies, review/intent revisions and expected predecessor. Unsupported contract versions fail unavailable; they are not stripped into an older schema.

A Client-only profile change or intake autosave is not a Case fact change and is invisible to workforce until explicitly selected sharing/submission. At that point a new submitted revision invalidates older unsigned review. Submitted facts, captured assessments, authored edit revisions and signed editions are never overwritten. Document upload, scan availability, evidence acceptance for an identified assessment, official-source verification and a Representative's signature remain distinct facts.

## Actors and common admission rules

All interactive actions require the tenancy contract's live component session/principal, verified identity and age gate, with server-derived actor/time. The same current-authority evaluator governs direct Convex and Nitro. A person who is also this Case's Client receives the Client projection and cannot combine workforce privileges to review/sign their own Case.

- **Client-own:** accept/decline the intended invitation; select Consent and data; save/submit own facts; answer clarification; withdraw; end their Case participation; read/export exact own Signed plans; make rights/dispute requests. No Client action creates a professional review or signs a plan.
- **Preparer:** assigned Staff or Representative with the explicit action and current `case.prepare` for those recipients/facts may prepare/submit attributed Case facts, run standard assessment, request clarification, propose edits and request review. They cannot edit the global profile, attest for the Client, widen sharing or release unsigned output. Verification-pending/stale Representatives may prepare if otherwise permitted and not held.
- **Reviewer/signer:** an assigned Representative performs professional review under current `case.prepare` and `case.review_and_deliver` for that named reviewer and selected work. Signing additionally requires a currently usable selected verification, no professional-work hold, the exact candidate/dependencies and the full interactive ceremony. Missing/refused good-standing attestation denies. The 30-day check ends earlier at any known cutoff; equality at expiry denies.
- **Case manager:** an already authorized Representative with explicit Case-management action may manage permitted assignments and close work. Staff can request these changes; Practice administration alone can only nominate/propose, without Case contents. Adding recipients always needs affirmative Client selection.
- **Record custodian:** independent record-specific retained grants govern custody/read/export after live work ends. No signature, live intake or new advice follows. Replacement/continuity uses the consent decision's nomination, non-self approval and bounded operator-recovery path.
- **Service executor:** a scheduler/worker/callback acts on one stored operation/attempt and records both executor and original initiator. Recheck its current scope before protected reads, effects and finalization. No copied human JWT, caller actor, admin key impersonation or worker signature.

Unknown grants/policy, mismatched parents, withdrawn required scope, closed work, stale revisions or unavailable admission deny before payload loading. Client withdrawal/status/rights paths remain available when a newer Consent text is declined or its policy is unavailable. Client-own signed retrieval and retained retrieval use their separate policies; losing live sharing does not deny them automatically.

## Transition catalogue and atomic events

Every named event below uses the common audit envelope in the next section. Arrows change only the named axis. Other axes keep their identity and current warnings. Required state, pointer/grant/cancellation changes, operation receipt, audit events and notification/work intents are one Convex commit; any failure rolls them all back. Large immutable packages are durably staged/checksummed privately before admission. No external mail, object-store or authentication endpoint is claimed to join that transaction.

For a large cancellation fanout, the authoritative root cancellation epoch and its aggregate event commit together; all dependent admissions/finalizations read that epoch immediately. Bounded child cleanup and receipts may follow. Enumerating every job or writing every child event is never a prerequisite to making withdrawal, closure or deletion deny access.

No Case/profile/work grant. Transport failure changes only delivery receipt. |

An answer save alone does not submit/share it. Explicit corrected submission makes a new snapshot and replaces the unsigned cycle as specified below; old review is invalidated, not silently resumed. |

Staff can track/prepare receipt; only that Representative records their acknowledgment and disposition. Service/email receipt is not human acknowledgment. Failure/outcome unknown preserves progress and direct contact. No event manually clears escalation. |

| Factual/integrity/authority dispute → separate notice/restriction | Client-own rights request, or currently authorized Case/retained/rights operation with exact documented scope. Legal effect/notice duty is pending counsel. |

Signing is also portal delivery of the exact edition under the settled signature contract. `plan.made_available` records that delivery boundary; email acceptance and view acknowledgment record later, different facts. The original invitation's email receipt and a new plan notice cannot substitute for it. A rule change after signing can warn that the delivered edition is historical without changing its signature or automatically notifying that immigration action is required.

At signing, create retained read/export grants only for currently active named assignees within the disclosed recipient scope, and custody responsibility for the signer. These are exact record-set grants under the independent retained policy, not grants to all Practice members or rights to the Client's live sources.

Recording a source-byte difference alone permits a checking task, not a verified policy-change claim or known immigration impact. `change_known` identifies an evidenced change to the recorded basis; its effect on advice may still be undetermined. Dependency matching, potential matches and impact classification remain with the existing rule-change ticket. After live Consent ends, any retained warning/rights work needs its own narrowly recorded retained purpose and dependency references; a rules administrator or index job gains no live Case-content access from this workflow.

The intake gate stays `required` for status expiry within 90 Toronto calendar days (including today), expired status, refusal/removal/criminality/health, or `undetermined` for unresolved safety facts. Resume, submission, correction, processing/review admission and boundary timers re-evaluate current routing. Human acknowledgment/signature never clears it; permitted signed content remains factual limitations/referral without generated strategy. The five excluded subjects retain their out-of-scope route. Language conversion can be supported only for its evidenced criterion while program coverage remains unknown; it does not clear escalation.

### Authority changes, closure and retention

| Transition | Actor and preconditions | Atomic event and result / failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Active Consent → narrowed, withdrawn or expired scope | Client withdraws any purpose/document/whole Case; valid timed policy expires through expected-revision worker. No Practice approval or completed export required. | `consent.changed`, `consent.withdrawn`, `consent.expired` plus `work.cancelled` and `signature.intent_invalidated` for dependent work. Root revision/fence stops new admissions immediately, with durable bounded cleanup/cancellation intents. | Assignment/membership/Practice/professional authority → removed/held | Authorized membership/platform/verification operation, or assigned Case manager within current recipient scope; explicit current revisions and mandatory audit. | `assignment.deactivated`, `membership.changed`, `practice.held`, `professional_work.held` as applicable, with dependent work/dispatch fences. A stale lookup alone denies signature but permits otherwise authorized preparation; known adverse status applies the separate workforce/retained hold. Never automatically demote to Staff or transfer to another Practice. |
| Replace/add live assignee or signer | Authorized Case manager proposes; Client explicitly includes new named recipient/data/actions. If nobody can manage after departure, use the Client's valid connection/Consent path. Administrator nomination alone grants nothing. | `assignment.proposed`, `consent.changed`, `assignment.activated`; fence old work and intents, preserve authorship and prior signatures. | Withdrawn/expired sharing → renewed live work | Client affirmatively selects current text/facts/recipients and attestations for this still-open, nondeleting Case; active Practice/members, no applicable hold, explicit new assignments/actions. | `consent.granted`, `assignment.activated`, `revision.opened`; fresh receipt/cycle/work request. | Open → closed | Client chooses to end/close own Case; or currently authorized managing Representative closes under current Case action/Consent. Staff may request closure. Closure time is an application event, **not a decision about professional file closure or the start/duration of a statutory retention period**. Counsel supplies `<FILE_CLOSE_RULE>`, professional schedules, Client retrieval windows, Consent validity, rights deadlines and hold/transfer rules through the existing legal-floor ticket. Missing policy denies its affected retained operations/real-data activation; it never means retain forever. Viewing, export, reopening and rechecking do not restart an old retention clock. Finite named synthetic policies prove the mechanism without adopting legal durations.

Deletion/withdrawal of a shared document version also fences its live uses/copies; a new version is never shared automatically. The signed material-evidence projection can remain as its disclosed retained exception, without retaining raw vault bytes by default. An erased Client account has no active portal entitlement: further requests use the verified rights path, and a later signup with the same email cannot rebind ownership. Practice closure/member departure follows bounded custody continuity, never a general administrator data bypass.

Erasure completion is relative to the exact request manifest and its disclosed exceptions. `erasure.completed_live` requires reconciled eligible live-store receipts; final `erasure.completed` additionally requires the known recovery-copy obligations to have expired/be verified. Retained/held exceptions remain separately listed, never described as erased. Unknown stores or failed cleanup keep completion pending, without delaying the initial access-denial fence.

## Minimal audit contract

Each committed event records:

- Server-created event ID, schema/type, environment, opaque Practice/Case/record-set identifiers as applicable, and a monotonically advancing per-aggregate sequence. Proposal events use proposal ID before a Case exists; no placeholder Client/Case is invented.
- Actor kind (`client`, `staff`, `representative`, scoped operator or service), stable principal and actual role/action at admission. Service events separately identify executor, originating principal/operation or approved schedule; a worker is never logged as the human signer. Actor identity is minimized to accountability requirements, not a copied account/profile.
- Authoritative server UTC commit time, operation/request ID and causation/correlation references. A reported observation/effective time is separate, precise only when known; missing historical dates stay unavailable. Sequence/revision resolves ordering when timestamps coincide.
- Previous/next state codes and aggregate revisions; exact relevant input, assessment, edit, plan, source/rule/check, Consent/text/scope, membership/assignment, verification/hold and policy revisions/digests. Signature events reference the complete sealed receipt rather than copying it into every event.
- Minimal bounded outcome/reason code, immutable evidence/receipt references and checksums, predecessor/supersession link, and any outbox/job/attempt/fence reference. Changed field IDs can explain a correction without storing before/after personal values. Sensitive triage reasons stay in the restricted Case task; general audit/notification projections use a generic human-review reason and the task reference.

Do not put names/contact addresses, document titles/bytes, immigration answers, full before/after records, narrative medical/criminal/refusal content, raw assessment/results, Staff notes, passwords/tokens, IP/device histories or message bodies in general audit payloads. Necessary signer name/licence/attestations and as-used facts belong in their already-governed signature/retained evidence, not a universal event dump. Opaque IDs and digests can still identify people; apply approved access and retention schedules to them.

The transactional helper receives server-derived context inside each domain mutation; no public `appendAudit(actor, timestamp, arbitraryPayload)` service supplies authority. Required audit failure aborts the transition, including signature/publication and access-admission intents. Rejected operations create no success event or partial pointer; bounded security diagnostics may record a non-enumerating denial without leaking guessed foreign identifiers/content. Append corrections/dispositions, never mutate or delete a historical event except through the approved retention/rights process. This is controlled append-only history, not event-sourced rebuilding, provider WORM or cryptographic non-repudiation.

Client history includes their own consent, submissions/origins, task/service/human receipts, signature/publication, permitted view, warnings and rights outcomes. It excludes unsigned professional content and custody/security dossiers. Assigned workforce gets the authorized Case projection; independent retained admission gets its allowed sealed record and custody/audit. Practice administrators get membership/nomination operations; cadet2 admins get verification/security/rules and minimal operational states. No global Case audit list/count leaks. Live queries read current authority dependencies and do not manufacture audit entries; view/retained/download admission is an audited mutation followed by still-guarded retrieval, not a reusable bearer grant.

## Portal status contract: four kinds, never one verdict

Every status names its Case, selected edition/cycle revision, observation/as-of time, next actor and available action where applicable; pre-Case statuses name only the proposal. Denied/unavailable is distinct from a successful empty state. Work status never implies eligibility, probability, complete evidence or professional review.

The table uses these precise projections to avoid exposing Draft content:

- **C0:** assessment coverage is not disclosed because no Signed plan is available. This is a disclosure/work placeholder, not a fifth finding status or an `unknown` assessment of a person.
- **CS:** the selected accessible Signed plan's exact per-subject `supported` / `blocked` / `unknown` / `out_of_scope`, reasons and scope, plus separately labelled current warnings. It never comes from the new unsigned cycle. If no plan exists, use C0.
- **P0 / PD / PS:** workforce coverage is respectively not yet assessed, the authorized exact Draft's per-subject findings, or the selected Signed plan's historical findings. Processing failure is unavailable, not blocked. PD and PS can coexist, each labelled with its revision.
- **EC / EP / ES:** EC is Client-own supplied/uploading/scan-pending/available/awaiting-review/replacement-needed evidence and permitted requests/actual acceptance receipts, without Draft conclusions. EP adds assigned workforce's exact fact/document review, accepted-for-assessment revision, source verification and unresolved evidence limits. ES is the signed as-used evidence/provenance/dates, with current stale/withdrawn/unavailable warnings separately. Upload/available is never accepted; acceptance is specific to the identified assessment, not universal validity.

Within each audience cell the order is **assessment coverage; work status; evidence status; review validity**. These are semantic labels, not visual design. Rows with an earlier plan use CS/ES even while a private new cycle is active. Restricted payload fields stay masked according to the current audience policy.

## Concurrency, idempotency and recovery

Each interactive command carries an opaque operation key, expected aggregate revisions and canonical request digest scoped to environment, principal, Case/proposal and action. Store its receipt in the domain mutation. An identical retry returns the original committed IDs/outcome only if the caller may currently read it; a changed payload under the key conflicts. A timeout is pending/unknown until the operation receipt is recovered, never a success or permission to create a second request. Withdrawal/rights retries expose only the permitted minimized outcome. Keys/receipts have a governed horizon sufficient for queued/replayed work; expiry cannot permit an old event to be re-executed as new.

Expected revisions serialize competing saves/submissions, the one current unsigned cycle and current-plan/predecessor pointer. Conflicts return a bounded authorized comparison and require explicit resolution; no last-write-wins or silent merging of facts/professional edits. An unchanged retry makes no second domain transition/event/notice. Uniqueness checks are transactional, not an assumption that an index is unique.

| Race | Required outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Sign versus edit/new submitted input/source-check or warning change | Change first invalidates the candidate/intent; sign fails. Sign first preserves its exact edition; the later change produces a separate revision/review-needed warning. An unshared profile autosave is not a signed dependency change. |
| Two signers or two candidates | One signature per candidate and compare-and-swap of shared predecessor/current plan. One commits; the other gets already-signed/conflict and must reopen review. Never co-sign, bulk-sign or silently replace a different candidate. |
| Sign versus withdrawal/close/member removal/credential hold | Denial first means no plan/retained package; sign first preserves only its exact independent signed/retained rights. Later denial cancels dependent unsent dispatch and live work, without erasing the Client's permitted own plan. |
| Old worker versus corrected input, reassignment, cancellation or new cycle | Finalization checks stored operation, cycle/input, authority and attempt fences again; stale output cannot become current. Preserve only minimized failure/cancellation receipts under schedule. One logical processing claim is never a caller-controlled retry mode. |
| Expiry versus renewal or idle view | Server time and expected authority revision apply on every action. Scheduled expiry updates reactive fences; old expiry cannot expire a newer receipt/check. DTO validity bounds clear clients at cutoff/disconnect; delayed scheduling cannot admit expired work. |
| Delivery callback versus withdrawal/cancellation | A correlated authenticated callback can annotate the historical effect admitted earlier, never reopen cancelled intent, advance Case/plan state, expose payload or send again. |
| Erasure/hold versus export or retained read | Root restrictions and current manifest/policy are read at admission/publication/download. Restriction first denies; previously admitted bytes may already be in flight. An allowed hold preserves only named records and does not restart work. |

External work has a durable intent, exact initiating authority, bounded claim/lease, monotonically changing attempt fence and per-effect receipt. Recheck before each protected read, before dispatch admission and before publication/finalization. Logging out does not cancel a previously admitted durable business operation; losing its required principal/member/Consent scope does. Rights cleanup uses the independent committed rights manifest, never the deleted Client's old session.

The send call is outside Convex. Commit dispatch admission first; a timeout/crash after dispatch may have begun is `send_unknown`, with no automatic resend or new idempotency key. Reconcile authenticated provider evidence; only proven nonacceptance permits bounded retry. Distinguish `captured_synthetic` (not sent), `provider_accepted`, `delivered_to_mail_server`, `failed`, `suppressed`, `retrying` and `send_unknown`. Do not promise exactly-once external delivery or recall of already admitted/downloaded bytes. Artifact/export workers cannot mutate the authoritative signed edition.

Restore in isolation with reads/signing/sends disabled. Replay the independently recoverable Canadian deletion/revocation/hold journal through its completeness watermark, then reconcile auth, bytes, pointers and pending intents. Missing completeness keeps the environment closed. Old snapshots/callbacks/imports cannot restore withdrawn Consent, assignments, expired intents, deleted ownership or a human signature. Historical audit imports retain source store/ID, original actor/time/provenance and explicit unavailable metadata; never manufacture a past Case/signature from legacy user/run identity.

## Glossary additions

These newly resolved terms are recorded here for the glossary owner; `GLOSSARY.md` is not edited.

- **Connection proposal:** A Practice's invitation for an identified Client to consider one Case relationship and its sharing choices. Acceptance alone shares no information.
- **Work cycle:** The preparation and review of one proposed plan for a Case, ending in a Signed plan or in supersession/cancellation of that unsigned work. A later revision is a new cycle.
- **Client edition:** The exact content a Representative reviews for disclosure as a Signed plan, including its stated facts, evidence, limitations and attribution.
- **Review need:** A recorded reason to reconsider work or a Signed plan because its basis changed, is disputed or cannot be confirmed. It does not itself establish the effect of that change or replace the plan.
- **Portal publication:** Making one exact Signed plan available to its Client under their access rights. Notification and viewing are separate events.
- **Case closure:** Ending live work on a Case while preserving separately permitted Client retrieval, rights requests and Practice-retained records. Closure alone means neither erasure nor an immigration outcome.

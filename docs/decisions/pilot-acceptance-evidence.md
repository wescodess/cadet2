## Evidence model and decision rule

There are four distinct proof locations. **CI** proves deterministic contracts, built-surface guards and real isolated-backend authorization/concurrency. **Staging** proves the deployed Canadian configuration, real managed backend/auth, actual bytes/scanner/capture, browser journeys and operational rehearsals. **Owner** proves visual approval and the listed human-review verdicts. **Real users** supply later observations of comprehension and usefulness; they are not manufactured by automation or owner approval.

Each evidence entry contains: criterion/seam/journey IDs; source version and independent expected result; source and QA-tree SHAs; app image/build digest, Convex function/schema/auth version and deployment identity; environment/origins/region and flag revisions; policy/legal/design versions; synthetic fixture IDs and clock basis; command or manual steps, runner/browser/viewport/theme/network; start/end time, exit and observed outcome; sanitized immutable artifact URL/path; reviewer and any bug/waiver link. Include the previous compatible artifact and denial-journal watermark for recovery evidence. Rebuild or materially changed backend, policy, flags or renderer invalidates affected evidence; source SHA alone is insufficient.

| Evidence state        | Meaning and effect                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Passed                | The named check actually ran on the named artifact/configuration, met its sourced expectation and retained its evidence. A passing denial has a permitted positive control. Its scope is only that check and matrix cell. |
| Failed                | It ran and disagreed, crashed, timed out or produced incomplete/unrecognized output. Record the observed failure and bug. A tool failure is not a clean scan.                                                             |
| Blocked               | It could not run because an identified prerequisite is absent, such as account access, Canadian runner proof, a participating professional or approved UI. Name the dependency and owner. No pass is inferred.            |
| Unrun                 | No attempt was made. Planned, configured and statically inspected checks remain unrun runtime evidence.                                                                                                                   |
| Supplied              | A prior record/run was provided and its provenance is linked. It is not a new run on this candidate; count it only where its exact immutable scope still applies.                                                         |
| Skipped / quarantined | Record the reason and ticket; neither is coverage. A retry that happens to pass does not erase a failure or establish a stable journey.                                                                                   |
| Not applicable        | Only for a demonstrably absent capability outside the declared scope, with reason and reviewer. It cannot hide a required B1–B7 surface or substitute for blocked evidence.                                               |

Maintain a finite traceability ledger under QA ownership: destination criterion → surface and inherited state → seam → independent journey or justified manual check → exact run. Store plans in `qa/plans/`, acceptance journeys in `qa/acceptance/`, fixtures in `qa/fixtures/`, sessions in `qa/sessions/`, and sign-off in `qa/signoffs/<candidate>.md`. Evidence files stay outside git at `~/.orchestrate/evidence/cadet2/<candidate>/` or approved CI artifact URLs. Canadian private-evidence placement and redaction follow FG; public reports contain no credentials, tokens, private register dossiers or real Client data. Preserve the necessary pilot packet before CI's 14-day artifact expiry.

QA's fresh sign-off names the complete artifact/configuration, all required matrix cells, scope/flags, every human verdict, rehearsals and open bugs. It requires zero open S1/S2 bugs in scope, zero flaky journeys counted as coverage, every budget met and every criterion passed. Missing evidence is a failed sign-off check even when its underlying run is classified blocked or unrun. Only the owner can record a reasoned waiver of QA NO-GO under QA policy; the orchestrator cannot. A waiver does not turn the failed check green or authorize real data, foreign processing or excluded product scope.

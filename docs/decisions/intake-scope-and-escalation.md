## Entry points and the minimum question set

Account identity is reused through admitted server records; do not ask for first name, last name or email again. Show the named Client and, where authorized, Practice/recipients so a wrong person can stop. A wrong, expired or cancelled invitation has no intake access. Connection acceptance and terms acknowledgement alone share no facts. Public information and the [CICC register](https://register.college-ic.ca/Public-Register-EN/RCIC_Search.aspx) remain accessible without a complete intake.

An explicit unknown or request to answer with the Representative counts as an answer, never as clearance. | Education/employment history, money, language results, documents, all five legacy sections and a clear safety gate. Reminder scheduling, file constraints and provider choices remain with [Define solo Client utilities and reminder behavior](https://github.com/wescodess/cadet2/issues/41). Its inputs above are an intake dependency contract, not a second vault/reminder specification. Adding a solo fact/file to a Case is a new explicit selection of the exact version. Creating a Case never copies a whole solo vault, date list or profile.

Solo tracking evaluates only the date-based attention state of an explicitly labelled status item: imminent/expired dates offer the human route, and an unknown applicable date offers clarification/human help. It does not create a Case EscalationGate or demand the four sensitive safety answers. A status item beyond 90 days has no date-based trigger; that is not a claim that the person's overall safety gate is clear. General help remains available without supplying sensitive facts.

## Question order, branching and content

This order fixes the dependencies of questions, not their screen layout or number of pages. Back/change actions preserve answers and re-evaluate dependent questions.

1. **Purpose and authority, before sensitive collection.** Identify this Client, the Case/Practice and named recipients, what preparation/review will do, the signed-only boundary, storage/sharing/retention and withdrawal consequences. Require the versioned 18+ attestation. Offer separate unchecked `case.prepare`, `case.review_and_deliver`, selected `case.documents` and `case.reminders` choices exactly as the consent decision defines. Declining preparation leaves an unshared connection and solo utilities. Declining review/delivery can leave authorized preparation, without signature or Client assessment output. A Client can narrow which sensitive facts are shared; unanswered/unshared facts stay unknown. Only synthetic, conspicuously pending-counsel text is used for this pilot.
2. **Urgency first.** Ask whether temporary Canadian status applies or is uncertain, its reported type and applicable expiry if relevant. Ask separately about refusal, removal, criminality and health, with **Yes / No / I don't know / I prefer to discuss this with my representative**. Explain each term in plain English without asking the Client to determine legal admissibility. Each affirmative answer immediately offers the human route, without requiring the rest of the intake. Missing facts offer clarification or the same route. Do not request a refusal narrative, charge details or medical history.
3. **Scope next.** Ask what help is sought, then each excluded category explicitly. Do not infer a category from nationality, French language, family members, occupation, residence or free text. An excluded-only request ends the long questionnaire and offers referral. Unclear scope asks a structured clarification or hands off. Mixed requests retain separate subject boundaries. Urgency remains independently visible and gates the whole Case.
4. **Small, optional context for permitted work.** Offer province preferences and the optional reviewer-context fields below, with their specific purpose. An unknown/withheld value never prevents handoff. Request dependant authority before any identifiable dependant fact; otherwise collect, at most, the optional non-identifying count. Do not collect education/NOC/full household histories to feed an engine that does not use them.
5. **Language results when relevant and chosen.** Ask whether the Client has results, is awaiting results, has not taken a test, is unsure, or prefers to supply them to the Representative. Only an available result opens its exact test/version/report and per-skill questions. Keep English and French attempts separate. Earlier urgency/exclusion never forces a Client to complete this branch to obtain help; later evidence collection can resume it under the same current authority.
6. **Check answers, share exactly these facts, then receipt.** Distinguish known facts, unknowns, withheld/not-collected facts, invalid inputs and disputes; show origins and recipients. Allow changes without retyping unrelated answers. Submission confirms the facts being handed over, not their legal correctness, completeness, Consent to extra purposes or approval of advice. Receipt behavior is specified below.

Before an optional document request, state why this particular document is useful, whose it is, acceptable forms and that upload is not verification. Never ask for a provider login, language-test PIN, UCI, passport number, medical diagnosis or criminal narrative as part of this minimum intake. A language accommodation alone does not answer the separate health question.

## Fact representation and validation

Use the assessment contract's `InputFact` shape for assessment/scope/safety facts, including scalar `field`, `unit`, `state`, `value`, provenance, actor/time and authorized evidence references. Workflow controls such as Consent, attestations and submit/save receipts have their own records; they are not engine facts. Collection schema `intake.v1` and the amendment identifier below are explicit revision metadata, not claims of deployed code.

| State / answer | Stored and displayed meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `known` | Valid typed supplied value, including real `0` and `false`, with non-null provenance. No numeric-prefix parsing, inferred no, guessed date or guessed legal category. |
| `missing` | Asked but unanswered, **I don't know**, or **prefer to discuss**. `value = null`; keep a separate bounded answer-disposition code (`unanswered`, `unknown`, `withheld_for_human`) and the relevant clarification. It is not a negative response. |
| `invalid` | A supplied value fails syntax, range, enum or cross-field validation. `value = null`; retain the bounded original entry privately in the editable answer record and a specific error. Invalid input never reaches a calculator as a number. |
| `conflicting` | Two unresolved candidate facts or internally contradictory answers. `value = null`; reference the attributed candidates and correction task instead of silently choosing the last writer. |
| `not_collected` | Not asked in this entry point/branch, removed by policy, or not selected for Case sharing. `value = null`; distinguish `not_applicable`, `outside_purpose` and `not_shared` in collection metadata. It does not prove no/zero. |

For every known Client entry use `client_reported`, recorded Client principal and server commit time. For workforce entries use `staff_entered` with the actual preparing Staff/Representative actor; use `representative_checked` only for a documented check actually performed by a Representative. Keep the earlier supplied fact and check relationship so checking does not erase its origin. Evidence references are optional for reported facts and grant no automatic verification. Legacy values with no trustworthy provenance stay unresolved until recollected/checked; never backfill actor/time or call the Client the author of imported values.

Raw input and answer disposition live inside the same authorized data boundary, not logs, analytics or an audit payload dump. Assessment projections contain normalized scalar InputFacts; repeated goals, skills, statuses or people have separate fact IDs/paths. Arrays are collection envelopes, never smuggled into `InputFact.value`. Derived conversions are claims/receipts pointing to source facts, not fictional `client_reported` CLB facts.

Validation is authoritative on the server and shared with the client. Reject unknown field names and incompatible branch payloads. Drafts may retain incomplete or invalid entries safely; a submission can carry an invalid optional fact as `state = invalid`, `value = null`, with its correction reason and unknown assessment consequence. Preserve that state rather than relabelling it missing or silently dropping it. It cannot represent invalid data as known. Identity, valid current authority, required attestations, exact Consent selection and operation/revision binding are hard gates; factual uncertainty is not.

Use strict full-string finite decimal parsing, never `parseFloat` prefixes, NaN, infinity or silent rounding. Date-only values are real Gregorian `YYYY-MM-DD`, with no coercion of 31 February. Partial month/year dates have their own precision/raw entry and a missing day-level InputFact; never assign day 1 or a month end. Units below are canonical; no automatic currency conversion. Technical bounds are input limits, not legal thresholds.

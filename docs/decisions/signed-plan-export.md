# Signed plan export into a Practice's workflow

Decision date: 2026-11-17. Status: **answered-on-auto** for the private staging-pilot specification. Resolves [Define Signed plan export into a Practice's existing workflow](https://github.com/wescodess/cadet2/issues/44) under [Chart the Client and Representative portals for a staging pilot](https://github.com/wescodess/cadet2/issues/29). This records content, behavior and first test seams; it implements no exporter and establishes neither legal clearance nor pilot readiness.

**Export one explicitly selected Signed plan as an accessible PDF rendition, a structured UTF-8 JSON package, or complete copy-paste text.** A Practice receives the exact signed Client edition, its evidence index, both signing attestations, a permitted event receipt and the external matter reference. A Client can download their own matching edition and public signer evidence. Every format uses the same audience-authorized source manifest. Renditions never become the authority, release a Draft, refresh advice or confer access to a Case.

## Model and required contents

A **Client edition** is the exact disclosed content frozen for the Plan signature. A **Rendition** represents it in another format. An **Export package** combines the rendition/source representation with permitted evidence and receipts. The **Export receipt** describes the export operation; it is distinct from the Plan signature receipt and the Case event receipt. These concepts cannot be collapsed into a mutable “latest plan PDF.”

Export selects one stored Signed plan ID and revision, not a run ID, latest Draft, editable browser state or current profile. The package resolves one immutable Case–Practice–Client binding. Its signed advice, retained material evidence and historical signature are self-contained; current authority and warning records may be consulted without reading live Client facts. A new Draft or changed profile never changes the selected signed edition.

Every Practice format contains all five components below. PDF/text may render machine identifiers readably, but cannot silently omit a component, limitation or date to make the output shorter.

| Component | Required content and boundaries |
| --- | --- |
| **Exact signed Client edition** | Plan ID/revision, recorded Client and Practice attribution, stated scope, assessment/as-used date, exact advice/ordered tasks, included findings and adopted Representative edits with their recorded authorship/revisions, material as-used facts/provenance, missing facts, negative findings, scope/escalation limits and legal-text versions. System findings and authored professional judgment remain distinct. No current-template rewrite, normalization of reviewed wording or unsigned correction. |
| **Evidence index** | Link each included claim/finding/edit to its fact IDs, rule ID/version/digest/applicability/effective interval, citation ID, source title/canonical URL, retained passage/locator/excerpt/snapshot digest, publication/retrieval/effective dates, source-check reference/attribution and verified-as-of with its actual precision/status. Multi-source claims retain each check date. Include bound calculation method/version, inputs/units/steps/result, or explicit unavailable/not-applicable reason. Include only material retained evidence; document references give as-used version/checksum and verified facts, not raw vault bytes or a download grant. |
| **Signature receipt** | Export the authorized projection of the original receipt: historical professional name, RCIC/lawyer designation, selected licence number, regulator/jurisdiction, Practice at signing, signature/plan IDs, server signing instant/timezone, historical licence observation/status/check date and official verification link, plus source binding digests. Include **both exact accepted attestation texts, their separate versions/digests/status and acceptance**, adoption and current good standing. Show the recorded assurance/reauthentication method/time only where the projection permits; no credential, token, private verification dossier or internal session material. No current account name/licence substitution. |
| **Event receipt / audit summary** | Audience-safe timeline for this selected signed record: relevant submitted/reviewed revision references, signature, portal publication, recorded notice/view outcomes, later corrections/supersession/disputes, applicable Consent/custody events and export-admission reference. Give server event IDs/sequences/times, permitted actor/role attribution, bounded outcome codes and an explicit cutoff. A portal publication is not notice delivery; mail acceptance is not a Client view; a view is not agreement. Exclude Draft text, internal discussion, unrelated workforce/governance/security events and private hold details. Explain any policy-limited or unavailable history without inventing events. |
| **External matter ID** | The Practice's optional saved reference for the same Case in its existing case system, with its own recorded revision/as-of time and an explicit `not_set` when absent. Include it in Practice export metadata, separate from the signed advice and technical Case ID. It never grants access, identifies a different Client by lookup, or implies delivery to that external system. The policy below governs corrections and retained access. |

The authorized signature/evidence/audit projections may omit restricted internal fields. Verify the complete authoritative retained edition/manifest/receipt server-side **before** projecting. The JSON omission manifest records withheld field paths/classes and safe reasons; PDF/text explain material omissions. Carry the original `contentDigest`, `bindingDigest` and `recordDigest` with their defined scope, but do not claim a recipient can recompute a full receipt/manifest digest from a minimized projection. A digest of deleted raw inputs does not make them recoverable. Both attestation texts and all required Client-disclosed evidence/limitations must remain present; omission cannot disguise missing required signed material.

### External matter reference policy

Keep one optional bounded text reference per Case as Practice custody metadata, not a contact database or government identifier. Do not infer it from email, passport, UCI, free-text intake or a different Case. Only a currently authorized Case manager with an explicit metadata-write action and the necessary live scope may set/correct it during live work. After live sharing ends, a record custodian needs the explicit bounded custody-metadata action on the retained set, a custody purpose and a reason. Export permission alone permits neither metadata edits nor arbitrary per-request reference overrides.

Store reference changes as attributed revisions with audit; snapshot the selected saved revision into the export manifest. Retained export reads the retained custody metadata, never a live profile/Case-intake join. If a reference was recorded with the signed record, preserve it as the historical reference; a later correction is separately labelled with its time and does not change any signature digest. An export prepared before a reference change must be invalidated/rebuilt before publication. Do not use the value in URLs, object keys, filenames or general logs. It is personal/custody information under the record's schedule, not a public search key.

If the reference already appears in the frozen Client edition, preserve it there exactly; correcting that signed text requires a Superseding plan. A Practice-only reference added outside the edition does not appear in the Client download or allow a Client to edit Practice metadata. No reference is required to obtain a Signed plan or download it.

Content and accessibility are specified below; page layout, typography, colours and components await the UI gate. |
| **Structured JSON package** (`application/json`, UTF-8) | A versioned `signed-plan-export.v1` envelope contains the frozen Client edition using its original schema, authorized retained evidence and receipt projections, event receipt, external reference where permitted, legal-text/status references, source IDs/revisions/digests, generation/currentness cutoffs, component manifest/checksums, omitted/unavailable items and retention/expiry metadata. No abbreviated AI summary, selected unsupported claim, hidden rich-text-only limitation or silent truncation. A Practice may manually paste it into its existing system. The pilot exports one selected signed version at a time. It adds no bulk caseload export, ZIP of a whole Practice, direct third-party delivery, CRM, billing, integration, new e-signature provider or government filing. The broader Client-owned account export already specified by the consent decision remains separate: its JSON/private ZIP and available owned documents must reuse these signed-plan projections, not widen this Practice package to include raw documents or unsigned work.

An active Client account may download its **own selected Signed plan PDF**, with exactly the same edition revision, `contentDigest`, historical signer details, licence-check evidence, both attestation texts, disclosed claim evidence/dates and material limitations as that edition. Client-own JSON and text representations use the same content binding when offered directly or inside the existing account-rights export. Their event receipt contains only the Client-safe disclosure/Consent/rights history. Practice custody metadata, private signer verification evidence, internal authentication references, Staff notes and another person's restricted information are excluded. A Client who is also a Practice member receives this Client projection on their own Case, never a union of privileges.

A Client may retrieve a historical or Superseded plan where their rights permit, including after Case closure or Consent withdrawal; show the selected revision and separate current warning. Do not silently substitute the successor or include an unsigned replacement. Deleting the account ends ordinary portal authentication; subsequent access uses the consent decision's verified rights-request path. Signing up again with the same email does not recover ownership by inference.

**Never exported through any Signed plan format, including Practice output:** Draft assessments; Staff notes/internal discussion; unsigned editions, unadopted edits or replacement advice; full raw engine/input payloads or vault files; heuristic legacy scores, ranks, tiers, confidence, freshness or generated strategy; private licence/identity dossiers; credentials/session material; unrelated Cases or restricted audit. The exclusion applies to visible content, JSON fields, comparisons, metadata, hidden PDF layers/comments/attachments and clipboard text. Adopted text preserves its recorded preparer's authorship, but adoption does not release that person's other notes. The separate Client rights export can include their own entered facts and owned documents under its existing policy; it never gains an unsigned-assessment exception.

### PDF accessibility and faithful rendering

Treat accessibility as an output acceptance gate, not a claim inferred from the source page or the PDF library:

- Produce real, selectable/searchable Unicode text, a tagged semantic structure, document title/language, logical reading order, headings/lists and meaningful link text. Mark table headers and relationships wherever tabular data is used. Relevant graphics need equivalent text; decorative material is marked as such. No image-only printout, script-dependent reading or picture of a signature as the only signer evidence.
- Keep every limitation, citation locator, date, URL, signer detail and attestation readable and reachable by keyboard/assistive technology. Do not rely on colour, position, hover, an expandable web panel or an online-only tooltip to express a warning. Long URLs, long names, multi-page evidence and accented text cannot be clipped or lost at page boundaries.
- Embed the required fonts/assets locally in the Canadian renderer; no remote font/image/tracking request may disclose record contents. No hidden Drafts, comments, change history, arbitrary attachments, active content or identifying machine paths in PDF metadata. An accessibility permission setting cannot prevent text extraction by assistive technology.
- Validate actual generated synthetic PDFs using automated structural/text checks and manual keyboard/screen-reader reading, zoom/reflow where supported, and print/readability checks. Compare extracted text and citation/receipt coverage against independent expected content. Library support or an automated checker alone is insufficient; no PDF accessibility-conformance claim is made by this decision.

PDF accessibility failure makes that PDF failed/unavailable; do not publish it as accessible. An independently validated JSON/text request can still succeed with its own receipt and clear format-specific result. It never marks the failed PDF request successful. Owner approval of visual design remains necessary before the PDF surface is accepted for the pilot.

## Rendition binding, dates and currentness

Convex's immutable Signed plan, frozen Client edition, retained evidence manifest and Plan signature receipt remain authoritative. Reuse the signature decision's UTF-8 RFC 8785 canonicalization, SHA-256, schema versions and domain-prefixed `contentDigest`, `bindingDigest` and `recordDigest`; do not define another signing serializer. Changing PDF pagination or renderer version creates a different rendition, never a new signature or new source verification.

Every export manifest binds these semantic fields; names are contract notation, not a final route/table schema:

| Manifest group | Required binding |
| --- | --- |
| Source | Environment; stored Practice/Case/Client and retained-set IDs; exact plan/signature IDs/revisions; content/binding/record and retained-manifest digests; signed content/assessment/renderer schema versions; included evidence/edit references. |
| Audience and authority | Requester, Client-own/live-Case/retained branch, requested action and permitted purpose, current admission/authority/policy/restriction revisions, projection/omission version, operation ID. Internal session/fence details stay in protected metadata, not public receipt fields. |
| Current overlays | Authorized warning/supersession/dispute/credential-status references, revisions and observation/as-of dates; saved external-reference revision where permitted; audit cutoff event sequence/time; exact legal-notice versions/digests/status. Unknown warning impact is explicit. |
| Rendition | Requested format/media type, generator/template and projection versions, server generation time in UTC with displayed timezone, byte length and SHA-256 byte checksum, component checksum scopes, artifact/storage version, finite expiry and inherited retention class. |

The byte checksum of the completed PDF/JSON/text file is stored in Convex and its export/download metadata, **outside the bytes it hashes**; a file cannot contain its own whole-file checksum. Embedded plan checksums identify the signed source, not the surrounding PDF. JSON component checksums identify precisely the canonical components being hashed. Checksums detect content differences; they are not an additional professional signature, public authenticity service, regulator endorsement or proof of legal admissibility.

Keep these times separate: input submission, assessment/as-used, source retrieval/publication/effectivity, each claim's verified-as-of, licence observation/validity, signature, warning observation, event cutoff and rendition generation. Preserve unavailable dates and their reasons. Generation, download, printing and copying never reset a source check, licence check, review or retention clock. Never use the legacy retrieval-age freshness calculation or today's date to fill historical gaps.

The export includes the original frozen scope/limitations and legal text, plus a clearly separate present-status notice and the legal registry's `lf.plan-status.v1.en` historical-copy disclosure: **pending counsel**, “Copy generated {exported_at}. Later warnings may not appear here. Check the authenticated current record.” Resolve text/variants through that registry; this is not a second source of legal copy. Preserve the original Representative's advice attribution; do not put a platform-wide “not legal advice” label over the plan.

A safe authenticated record locator may identify this plan without embedding a secret, Client name or live content. Opening it always reauthorizes; it is neither a bearer/public verification link nor a promise that the reader has an account. Printed/offline copies cannot acquire later warnings or be recalled. The platform can deny future retrieval and append correction/supersession notices, not erase a copy already pasted, printed or downloaded. An already-admitted response may have bytes in flight.

Integrity failure or missing required signed content/receipt/material evidence blocks the entire artifact and opens an integrity review; do not render an “authentic original” with a warning. An explicitly omitted/deleted raw source listed as unavailable is different: the sealed material projection can remain complete and exportable. Never repair history from a live profile, vault, current source fetch, latest template or unsigned Draft. A lawful whole-artifact restriction yields unavailable/status/rights routes, not a redacted document represented as the exact signed original.

## Authorization, withdrawal and retained records

All paths use the tenancy evaluator and server-derived principal/time/relationships. The exporter cannot introduce an admin-key route, trust a caller's `userId`/role/object key or accept an opaque URL as permission. Authorize before loading protected payloads. Search, status, counts, history and export-ready subscriptions read the same current dependencies; cached readiness is not continuing authority.

| Requester / situation | Export admission |
| --- | --- |
| Assigned Staff or Representative during live work | Current admitted session/active principal and Practice membership; explicit Case assignment/export action; applicable current granular Consent for the selected facts/recipients and delivery purpose; available signed record and no applicable hold/restriction. A `case.prepare` grant alone is not an implicit export action. Unsigned material remains excluded even for a preparer who can read it in the portal. |
| Client on their own Case | Own signed read/export rights, historical Case binding and current rights/restriction policy; no Practice membership or renewed live Consent required. Only the Client projection. |
| After scope reduction, Consent expiry/withdrawal or Case closure | Cancel/fence dependent live export intents and unpublished renditions. Practice access must reopen through **a new independently admitted retained export**, with explicit current `retained.export` grant on that fixed record set, active same-Practice membership/Practice/principal, allowed custody/rights/complaint/legal purpose, schedule and hold/disclosure policy. No automatic conversion of an old live job. Client-own requests continue under their independent rights unless separately restricted. |
| Staff, pending/stale Representative or signer maintaining a record | Ordinary custody does not require a fresh licence check, but still requires the explicit retained grant/purpose. Known adverse professional status/security hold is checked and may deny; mere expiry of a cadet2 check does not assert licence expiry. Historical signing confers no continuing access. |
| Removed/suspended member, departed signer or disabled principal | Immediate denial of dependent generation/publication/new downloads, including a previously ready artifact, retained grants and unexpired JWT/intent. Rejoining or joining another Practice restores nothing automatically. Another currently granted custodian may request their own export. |
| Practice administrator or cadet2 admin by that capability alone | No plan/evidence/audit export. Custody replacement follows nomination plus non-self approval or the existing bounded operator recovery; no self-grant, general Case export or impersonation. |
| Retention due, deletion/restriction or hold | Recheck exact record/policy/hold revisions and server time. Expiry denies ordinary exports before physical cleanup. A hold preserves only its specified records and permitted access; it does not renew sharing or authorize new advice. Missing/unapproved policy denies the affected operation. |

Record the retained purpose and grant before reading bytes. Retained output reads only the sealed signed/material set and allowed custody/audit/current-warning metadata; it never requires a surviving live input, profile or raw document. Preserve the consent decision's record-specific grants created at signing and bounded reassignment policy. A never-signed Case has no retained Signed plan to export. New advice/corrections still need live authority and a fresh Plan signature.

Every new view of prepared text, publication, download, Range and retry rechecks current session/principal, correct audience, required membership/assignment/Consent or retained/own grant, expiry, hold/restriction, source integrity and relevant manifest/overlay revisions. A change invalidates affected ready files and rejects late responses from an old authority epoch. Background generation uses its recorded initiator/branch and service attempt fence; it cannot retain a human JWT or acquire a stronger branch after revocation. Logged-out interactive retrieval denies; an admitted durable operation may finish privately only while its separately recorded business authority remains valid.

## Generation, partial failure and export audit

Use the system-of-record pending-artifact pattern; no object-store write joins the Convex transaction:

1. **Admit:** an audited mutation resolves the selected source and current authority, records exact request digest/operation key, audience, purpose, source/overlay/reference manifest and a private generation intent. An identical authorized retry returns that operation; a changed request under the key conflicts. No audit commit means no admission or payload return.
2. **Generate privately:** a bounded service attempt rechecks authority before protected reads, verifies retained checksums, renders only allowlisted fields and validates that format. Stage under an opaque immutable object/version in the approved Canadian boundary. It cannot sign, update advice, fetch missing historical evidence or dispatch an email attachment.
3. **Publish ready:** after successful byte write/checksum/format checks, one mutation rechecks authority, root cancellation/attempt fence, expiry, restrictions and exact source/overlay/reference/policy revisions, then stores byte metadata, ready state and audit atomically. A concurrent supersession or new warning requires rebuilding the overlay for the **same selected signed version**, not switching to a newer plan. Orphans remain inaccessible and get cleanup/reconciliation intents.
4. **Retrieve/copy:** an audited session-bound download/text admission precedes payload delivery; its subsequent Canadian stream still reauthorizes. Distinguish server-admitted transfer, response completion/failure and unknown interruption. Clipboard preparation/delivery and a client-reported clipboard result are separate; the latter proves neither an external-system paste nor that a human read it. Keyboard/manual copying may have no observable completion.
5. **Expire/cancel/clean:** deny affected artifacts immediately on authority/source restriction or TTL cutoff, then reconcile deletion of temporary, current/noncurrent and incomplete copies. Reconcile provider failures; never report erased before the required receipts. Restore replays the independent Canadian revocation/deletion/hold journal before reopening files or jobs.

Operation states distinguish `requested`, `generating`, `ready`, `failed`, `cancelled`, `expired` and `unavailable`; a lost response remains outcome-unknown to the caller until its operation receipt is recovered. Delivery attempts have their own outcome. A render failure creates no usable artifact or success message, never a partial PDF missing its evidence/receipt. Missing required checksums, unsupported schema, clipped required text, private-store/configuration failure and PDF accessibility failure are explicit bounded reasons. A timeout does not create a second export or claim a successful download. A different valid format requires its own admitted request/result; no silent fallback changes what was requested.

Use the lifecycle audit envelope for export/custody events, with semantic events such as `export.requested`, `export.ready`, `export.failed`, `export.cancelled`, `export.download_admitted`, `export.transfer_recorded`, `export.copy_outcome_reported`, `export.expired` and `export.erasure_recorded`. Retained admission also records `retained.export_requested`. Store server event ID/sequence/time, operation/attempt/causation, actual initiator and service executor, environment, opaque scope/source IDs, requested format/audience/purpose, exact source/manifest/checksum/projection/authority/policy references, bounded outcome and safe reason. The protected export metadata can hold the specific reference revision; general audit does not copy the external matter value, advice, facts, names, document titles, attestation text, credentials or payload bytes. Minimized denied-request diagnostics cannot enumerate a guessed foreign Case.

The packaged event receipt has an explicit cutoff and identifies its own export request/admission. It cannot include a future ready/download event or its own whole-file hash recursively. The separate operation receipt subsequently reports generation/ready/transfer truth and byte checksum. A later authorized event-receipt export may include those prior events. Audit/storage failures roll back admission or ready publication and deny disclosure; an ordinary reactive query cannot append a trustworthy access event by itself. No success callback is accepted as new authority or as a signature.

## Canadian placement and rendition lifecycle

Canadian Convex stores authoritative export intents, grants, source manifests, receipts, digests and audit. Generate and validate in Canadian compute; store PDF/JSON/text bytes, temporary files, renderer scratch, metadata/logs, cleanup receipts, keys and recovery copies only within the approved Canadian environment boundary. Use the existing Canadian object-storage abstraction or verified Convex Canadian file-storage path, with private ACLs, encryption in transit/at rest and an authenticated Canadian download proxy. No global CDN/shared cache, public bucket, direct `getUrl()`/presigned bearer download, external PDF SaaS, ingestion/R2 bucket, analytics trace, remote renderer asset or LLM may receive Client content or identifying metadata.

## Glossary additions

These are proposed domain definitions for the later glossary reconciliation; `GLOSSARY.md` is not edited.

- **Client edition:** The exact content disclosed to a Client and adopted by a Representative in one Plan signature. Avoid: latest Draft, current profile, PDF master.
- **Rendition:** A representation of a particular Signed plan in another format, preserving its content and identifying the signed version it represents. Avoid: original signature, refreshed advice, authority.
- **Export package:** The permitted Signed plan edition, evidence and receipts assembled for one export audience and purpose. Avoid: entire Case archive, CRM synchronization, raw database export.
- **External matter ID:** A Practice's own reference connecting a Case to its existing case system. Avoid: government identifier, Client identity, access credential.
- **Export receipt:** The record of an export request and its actual generation and delivery outcomes. Avoid: Plan signature receipt, proof of external filing, proof of reading.

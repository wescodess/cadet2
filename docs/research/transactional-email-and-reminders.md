## Scheduling and idempotency

### Feasible scheduler choices

- **Convex, conditional candidate:** `runAt`/`runAfter` persist scheduled functions; scheduling from a mutation is atomic with its database writes. Scheduled mutations execute exactly once except developer errors; external actions execute at most once and are not automatically retried. Scheduling does **not** propagate auth. Cancellation prevents pending work but cannot stop work already started. Scheduled results are available for seven days, so retain a separate minimal business receipt. Cron supports recurring UTC schedules; use it as a bounded due-work/recovery sweep, not one global job per Client.[^convex-schedule][^convex-cron]
- **Placement/cost gate:** Convex documents `aws-ca-central-1`, all infrastructure powering a deployment in its selected region, **1.3× standard usage charges**, and region fixed after creation. That supports a Canadian scheduler candidate but does not independently resolve team/tier entitlement, every dashboard/log/backup/support path or existing deployment placement **[unverified]**. #30/#45 must provide that evidence; do not default preview/dev jobs to a US deployment.[^convex-region][^hosting-ticket]
- **Cloudflare Cron Triggers:** UTC recurring triggers execute on underutilized machines; changes can take up to 15 minutes to propagate and invocations can be missed during propagation. No Canada pin or exactly-once application-send guarantee established **[unverified]**. A data-free periodic wake-up could call a Canadian service **(proposal)**, but the Worker must not receive addresses, reminder IDs, due dates, message bodies or other client-linked data until placement is established.[^cf-cron]
- **Cloudflare Queues:** at-least-once delivery requires deduplication. Global storage/processing by default; restricted jurisdictions documented are **EU, US and FedRAMP**, not Canada. A queue jurisdiction does not restrict producer/consumer Worker execution. Therefore no client-linked reminder payloads or Email Sending event payloads in Queues under the present requirement **(analysis)**. Even opaque IDs can remain client-linked; they are not an automatic workaround.[^queues-delivery][^queues-location][^cf-events]
- **Queues limits/costs if reconsidered for data-free work:** maximum delay **24 hours**, default paid retention **4 days**, configurable to **14 days**; Free retention **24 hours**. ### Proposed application contract for #41/#45

The following is design analysis derived from the guarantees above, not implemented or tested behavior.[^convex-schedule][^queues-delivery][^ses-api]

1. **Authoritative data:** in the selected Canadian database store a Client-owned reminder ID, source (`user_entered` or `representative_confirmed`), original calendar date/local time, IANA timezone, resolved UTC occurrence, schedule revision, opt-in/preferences revision and minimal outbox state. Dates do not infer a legal deadline or status right. #41 must decide date-only defaults, daylight-saving ambiguity/nonexistent times, late reminders and expiry windows explicitly.[^map][^utilities-ticket]
2. **Atomic admission:** authenticated write verifies ownership/Case authority, persists reminder change and unique logical occurrence together, then schedules an internal mutation. Use a stable key such as `(environment, reminderId, scheduleRevision, occurrenceUTC, channel)`; enforce uniqueness transactionally, not by an in-memory check or a non-unique index alone. Keep recipient identity out of provider metadata where possible.
3. **Claim and recheck:** a bounded sweep/trigger atomically claims due rows with a lease and attempt ID. Before dispatch, recheck current account/email verification, preference, suppression, schedule revision, deletion and any applicable Case consent/access. A task queued before revocation is not permission to disclose later. Cancel old schedules **and** invalidate their revision; cancellation alone does not stop running work.[^convex-schedule]
4. **Minimal external side effect:** separate internal send action from the transaction; send one generic message to one current permitted address. Store provider message ID and attempt outcome. Reserve capacity for verification/reset traffic and cap reminder fan-out/rate/retries. Generate auth tokens through the selected auth contract with short expiry and single use; do not place them in scheduled reminder arguments or logs.[^code-auth][^auth-ticket]
5. **Ambiguous send is a real state:** if the provider accepts a message and the response/receipt write is lost, a database lease cannot prove whether it sent. With SES, Postmark or other unverified deduplication support, mark `send_unknown` and reconcile verified events/provider records; do not blindly resend on lease expiry. Where verified idempotency exists, reuse the identical payload/key **within** its documented TTL. A new attempt/key or retry after TTL can duplicate; retain the local receipt longer than provider deduplication windows.[^ses-api][^postmark-idempotency][^resend-idempotency][^brevo-idempotency]
6. **Feedback:** authenticate provider callbacks using the provider's actual supported method, reject forged input, deduplicate event IDs, bind provider message ID to the correct outbox occurrence, and handle late/out-of-order/duplicate events without regressing terminal outcomes. Maintain suppression and opt-out separately from transient retries. Hard bounce/complaint blocks further sending until a deliberate correction/re-consent flow. Never auto-clear suppression merely because the Client presses resend.[^ses-events][^ses-suppression][^postmark-webhooks][^sendgrid-webhook]
7. **Honest UI and rights:** distinguish scheduled, cancelled, skipped, submitted, delivery-to-mail-server, failed/suppressed and unknown; none means read or acted on. Export/delete and retention cover reminders, outbox, callback payloads, suppressions and logs, subject to the selected rights/audit contract. Minimize payload retention; retain bounded redacted receipts. A reminder failure cannot silently remove the deadline from the portal.[^opc-principles][^utilities-ticket][^cf-log]

## What reminder email must not contain

**Proposed minimization boundary for #41/#47**, based on the settled non-advice utility scope, sensitive-data analysis and OPC limiting-use/retention/safeguards principles:[^map][^trust][^opc-principles]
